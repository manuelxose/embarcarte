The system holds the course; the decision, the watchkeeping and the responsibility remain yours.

## 8. Features and capabilities

### Steering system
- **Physical control head at the helm:** engage and disengage, mode selection, course or angle adjustment, alarm acknowledgement and the emergency stop within reach.
- **Local control computer:** runs the guidance logic, alarms and logging; it does not power the motor directly and it accepts no steering command from the vessel network or the internet.
- **Independent motor-control electronics:** applies each profile's limits and stops the drive if the heartbeat from the control computer is lost.
- **Drive matched to the vessel:** selected after the vessel survey, within a documented and compatible drive family.
- **Steering position reference:** enables closed-loop control and end-stop protection where the installation allows it; otherwise an open-loop case with verified end-stop and stall protection is documented.
- **Calibrated heading sensor:** with a calibration and deviation procedure, and a heading-versus-COG plausibility check.

### Physical safety
- **Physical emergency stop:** removes motor power independently of firmware and of the control computer's state. It is tested at handover and at every maintenance interval.
- **Motor-power isolation:** a device that you operate to isolate the motor circuit; software never drives it.
- **Manual control always available:** the drive's manual release or clutch and the control head's standby mode return the helm to the person.
- **Loss-of-power behaviour:** the drive must be de-energised, the helm must be steerable by hand, and the system must not resume a mode automatically when power returns.

### Steering modes
- **Course holding:** holds the commanded course under supervision. It is the product's reference mode.
- **Wind-angle steering:** intended for the Sail edition, conditional on verification of the mode and on a compatible, calibrated wind source on your vessel. It is not delivered as equivalent to course holding.
- **Route or track following:** intended where an approved and verified local integration with MAREA NAV navigation planning exists, where position quality is sufficient and where the mode has been verified. It is not collision avoidance and not automatic navigation, and it is attributed to no edition until it is verified.

### Sensors and sources
- **Heading, position, wind and steering feedback:** each source is checked separately and shown with its state. A missing or stale source is shown as such; an invented value is never presented.
- **Loss of a source:** the affected mode degrades or disengages with a classified alarm; if the loss affects safe steering, the drive stops.

### Local control and access
- **No remote control:** no internet, cloud or mobile-app steering path exists or is planned. Status may be viewed in an authorised, read-only way.
- **Authenticated local access (intended):** the control plane is defined as local, authenticated and audited, with the owner's authorisation required for any service access. This architecture is not built yet.

### Records and maintenance
- **Installation record:** configuration, drive, sensors, checks performed, software versions and test results.
- **Local diagnostics:** source status, alarms, history and an exportable diagnostic bundle for support, without granting control of the vessel.
- **Maintenance plan:** season-start inspections, owner checks and a mandatory review after any work on the steering system, a grounding or an electrical modification.

**What MAREA PILOT does not include:** autonomous or unsupervised steering, collision avoidance, remote or cloud steering, mobile-app control, nautical charts, weather, AIS, certifications or a self-installation option.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**A coastal passage with the course held.** You leave shelter, settle the boat on course and engage course holding from the control head, with the crew on deck and you watching the helm and the surroundings. The system holds the course; you adjust, keep watch and decide. If heading or feedback fails, the system declares it and returns to manual steering; the emergency stop is within your reach at all times.

**Replacing an old autopilot with no history.** You bought the boat with a steering device you know nothing about: not its age, its wear or its calibration. The vessel survey reviews the steering system, the drive, the wiring and the helm station; the quotation states what is removed, what is installed and what is out of scope. The installation ends with a supervised sea trial and training in which you practise manual takeover and the emergency stop.

**Sailing with wind angle (Sail edition, conditional).** If your boat has a compatible, calibrated wind source and the wind-angle mode has been verified for your installation, you can ask for it to be included in the quotation. If any condition is not met, the mode is not delivered and you are told why. In no case does the system tack or gybe for you without supervision.

## 13. Compatibility
MAREA PILOT compatibility is determined **per vessel, per drive family, per steering type, per sensor and per firmware version**, and is published in a compatibility matrix. There is no universal compatibility: no vessel, drive or sensor is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **Steering system:** the survey determines the steering type, travel, mechanical end stops and manual release. What falls outside the scope is declared out of scope.
- **Drive:** only drives within the documented and verified family are accepted; no torque, current or force figure is published until it is measured and documented.
- **Steering feedback:** the configuration must allow a steering position reference or a validated open-loop case with end-stop and stall protection.
- **Heading, wind and position:** each source is checked separately, with its calibration and version. Signal K is not presented as a blanket compatibility guarantee.
- **NMEA 2000:** its status is unresolved in the programme; no NMEA 2000 certification or operational compatibility claim is made.
- **MAREA NAV:** route integration is an intention conditional on future verification and approval; it does not exist today.
- **No remote control path:** no public steering path from the internet, the cloud or a mobile app is offered or implied.

Before any quotation, the installation survey of your vessel is the step that determines which configuration is valid and what remains out of scope.

## 19. FAQ
**Is this an autopilot I can leave alone?**
No. MAREA PILOT is an assisted and supervised steering system: it requires continuous watchkeeping and the ability to take manual control at all times. There is no unsupervised mode and no remote steering.

**What exactly does the quotation include?**
Your vessel's quotation names every included and excluded item: control head, computer, motor-control electronics, drive, feedback, sensors, harness, safety elements, installation, commissioning, sea trial, training and documentation. That document is the only reference for what is included.

**Why do I need a vessel survey?**
Because a steering system depends on the steering type, the drive, the feedback, the space at the helm and the electrical installation. Without a survey there is no valid configuration and no honest quotation.

**Can I take manual control at any time?**
Yes — it is a design requirement: the control head has a standby mode, the drive has a manual release and the physical emergency stop removes motor power. Manual takeover is practised during training and at handover.

**What happens if power is lost?**
The drive must be de-energised, the helm must be steerable by hand, and the system must not resume a mode automatically when power returns: it requires an explicit engagement.

**What happens if a sensor fails?**
The affected mode degrades or disengages with a local alarm; a missing or stale value is shown as such and never presented as valid. If the loss affects safe steering, the drive stops.

**Will it work with my steering or my drive?**
Only if they are within the documented compatible family and the survey confirms it. There is no universal compatibility, and a drive without a manual release is not accepted.

**Can I steer it from my phone or from ashore?**
No. No remote or cloud steering path exists or is planned. Status may be viewed in an authorised, read-only way.

**Is it certified?**
No certification is claimed. The product is defined and not for sale, and its safety programme is pending bench tests, supervised sea trials, independent review and conformity assessment.

**How much does it cost?**
No price is published. Cost depends on the survey, the drive, the sensors, the installation and the delivered modes, and is communicated in a written quotation per vessel.

**Can I install it myself?**
No. Installation is always professional: there is no self-install edition and no plug-and-play configuration.

**What maintenance does it need?**
An installer-defined plan with owner checks and season-start inspections, plus a mandatory review after any work on the steering, a grounding, a lightning strike or a system modification.

**Can I move it to another boat?**
It requires a new survey and a new quotation before use on another vessel. The intended transfer procedure keeps the installation record with the boat.
