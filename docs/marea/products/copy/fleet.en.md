# MAREA FLEET — Product page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA FLEET: fleet management for sailing schools, charter, marinas and maintenance companies

## 2. Meta description
MAREA FLEET is the organisation software for professional fleets: vessels, people, roles, maintenance, documents and history in one place. Read-only telemetry. No remote vessel control. Product defined and not for sale.

## 3. Hero headline
One organisation, all your vessels, and a record that does not leave when the crew does.

## 4. Hero supporting statement
MAREA FLEET brings the fleet, the team, the roles, maintenance, documents and operational alerts into one workspace with real permissions and an activity record. It shows what is happening, what is due and what was decided; when it receives authorised telemetry it presents it as read-only data with its source and age, and it never shows a vessel that has stopped reporting as online.

## 5. Main value proposition
Small and mid-sized fleets are not run on a deep-sea shipping ERP, and they are not run on a single-user app either: they are run on spreadsheets, messages and one person's memory. MAREA FLEET is defined for that gap: a sailing school, a charter operator, a marina, a maintenance company or a small workboat fleet that needs to know which vessels it has, who may see each one, what maintenance is due, which document expires and what happened on each vessel. The platform models the organisation — members, roles, assignments, invitations and audit — instead of sharing a password, and it keeps one operational record per vessel that survives crew changes, charter hand-overs and yard visits.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **The organisation, not the lone user.** Owner, administrator, fleet manager, maintenance manager, crew, read-only viewer, external installer and support have different permissions enforced on the server.
- **One record per vessel.** Equipment, tasks, work orders, documents, incidents and events stay attached to the same vessel.
- **Documents and expiry in view.** Every certificate or policy has an issuer, number, validity and version; an expired item is shown as expired.
- **Honest, read-only telemetry.** Where an authorised source exists, data appears with its source, timestamp and age; without data the vessel is shown as offline, never as a green tick.
- **Per-vessel cost that reconciles.** Each vessel has its own subscription, start date, renewal and cost owner.
- **The data is yours and can be exported.** The organisation can export its information and review who accessed what.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA FLEET is defined for organisations that operate several vessels and share shore staff. It combines four layers that must be understood separately:

- **The platform**, the software product: organisation, members, roles, vessel registry, profiles, maintenance, work orders, history, equipment, documents, incidents, events, assignments, notifications, reports, export and audit.
- **The per-vessel subscription**, which grants a time-limited right to use the platform for the contracted vessels and edition.
- **Read-only telemetry**, which exists only if the organisation authorises a compatible source and which never allows steering the vessel.
- **Services**, such as onboarding, data migration or training, which are quoted separately.

MAREA FLEET is not a remote-control system, not a distress service, not a security or regulatory compliance certification, and it does not guarantee a service level. **Product status:** complete definition (DEFINED), not available for purchase. This page describes the intended product; it is not a commercial offer, it contains no prices and it promises no availability.

## 8. Features and capabilities
**Organisation and access.** Organisation creation with identity verification and a verified first administrator; single-use invitations that expire; immediate revocation; eight roles with published permissions; organisation-level and vessel-level permissions; scoped, time-bound and audited access for external installers; a limited boat-owner view where the organisation chooses to grant it.

**Fleet and status.** Multi-vessel registry with identifiers, type, dimensions, home berth and operational state; a profile per vessel; a fleet overview with open work, expiries and telemetry freshness; availability and operational state with a reason; read-only states where an authorised source exists.

**Maintenance and history.** Recurring and one-off tasks with intervals and due windows; planned-maintenance scheduling; work orders with priority, assignee, parts and labour; service history per vessel and per equipment item; equipment and installed-component inventory; technical incidents with severity, symptoms and resolution; operational event history.

**Documents and notifications.** Certificates, insurance, vessel documentation and manuals with issuer, number, validity and version; expiry reminders and escalation to the responsible role; internal assignment and due notifications; operational alerts derived from authorised telemetry, with source and age.

**Shared work.** Activity assignment to a person or role with vessel scope; in-product notifications and configured channels; operational and management reports; scheduled reports.

**Control and trust.** Export of the organisation's data; audit record of access and changes; retention and deletion under the published policy; transfer of a vessel or account with verifiable consent; authorised, limited and revocable integrations.

**Language and documentation.** User interface, notifications, reports and documentation in Spanish and English.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**A sailing school with twenty boats.** The school registers its vessels and instructors, assigns boats to courses and uses an end-of-day checklist to catch defects. One boat is taken out of service after a fault; the incident is recorded, turned into a work order and kept in the history. When an instructor leaves at the end of the season, their access is revoked and the vessel record remains.

**A charter operator with a mixed fleet.** The operator manages owned and third-party vessels. Each owner receives a limited view of their own vessel; crew see only assigned vessels; the office reviews fleet status before each departure. The charter hand-over is documented and the owner receives a report without the office preparing it by hand.

**A marina serving customer vessels.** The marina registers its customers' vessels, authorises an external installer for a specific job with access limited to that vessel and that week, and keeps the incident and the work performed. When the customer leaves, the marina revokes access and exports its record.

**A maintenance company with several owners.** The service manager assigns work orders to technicians, records parts and hours, and gives each owner a report generated from the same history. The audit record shows who changed each order and when.

## 13. Compatibility
MAREA FLEET compatibility is published **per source, per device, per firmware version where applicable and per data direction**. There is no universal compatibility and no device is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **MAREA WATCH:** may supply read-only telemetry and alerts where a WATCH service exists and the organisation authorises it. Buying FLEET does not create a WATCH service.
- **MAREA HUB:** may supply installation health and read-only telemetry where authorised. FLEET does not configure or update the HUB and does not write to the vessel network.
- **MAREA AIS:** receive-only traffic and position context where the corresponding data-rights position exists; it does not imply collision avoidance or a transponder.
- **MAREA SENSE:** only through a verified WATCH/HUB path; SENSE has no connectivity of its own and its values are only valid within each module's verified range and calibration.
- **MAREA NAV and MAREA DISPLAY:** not included; FLEET is not a navigation product and not a control surface.
- **MAREA WEATHER:** licensed data with a separate entitlement; it is not included and is never billed twice with NAV.
- **MAREA PILOT:** at most a read-only status view after the relevant review; no steering command exists or is planned from FLEET.

Before any intended purchase, a fleet and data-source assessment determines which integrations would be valid and what is out of scope.

## 14. How it works
1. **The organisation is created** and its identity and first administrator are verified.
2. **Vessels are registered** and their operational profiles completed.
3. **Roles are designed** and people are invited with the appropriate vessel scope.
4. **Maintenance and documents are configured** with intervals, owners and expiry dates.
5. **Authorised telemetry is connected** where a compatible source exists, accepting the data-processing terms.
6. **Notifications and reports are tested** before onboarding is declared complete.
7. **The fleet is operated and reviewed:** the fleet view, due work, reports and audit record show what is happening; the organisation exports its data whenever it needs to.

## 19. FAQ
**What exactly does MAREA FLEET manage?**
The organisation, its vessels, its people and its work: fleet registry and profiles, maintenance, work orders, history, equipment, documents and expiry, incidents, events, assignments, notifications, reports, export and audit. Telemetry is read-only data where an authorised source exists.

**Is it a remote-control or steering product?**
No. There is no rudder, heading, motor, thruster, firmware or bus command, and no cloud-to-actuator path, and none is planned. Telemetry is read-only and never allows steering the vessel.

**Does it guarantee that an alert arrives instantly?**
No. Alerts depend on power, network coverage and delivery by the notification provider. Provider acceptance is not delivery, and delivery is not human acknowledgement; where there is no evidence, the state is shown as unknown.

**What service level does it guarantee?**
None. No service-level commitment and no published measurement exist. A service commitment could only exist in a signed contract with real measurement, and none exists today.

**Is it certified?**
No. There is no security certification (no ISO/IEC 27001, no SOC 2, no other), no classification-society approval and no regulatory-compliance certificate. The security model is specified in the tenancy and security contract and will be tested before any sale.

**Where is our data and who sees it?**
In the organisation's tenant, with a published retention policy, role- and vessel-based access, audit and export. Only members the organisation authorises can see the vessels, with immediate revocation. One organisation cannot read or modify another's data by design, and that separation will be tested before any sale.

**Can we use it without MAREA hardware?**
Yes. The platform stands alone; telemetry requires a compatible, authorised source.

**How many vessels, users and API calls are included?**
Limits will be published with the approved offer as concrete numbers or ranges. There is no unlimited-use promise.

**What happens if we cancel or change provider?**
The organisation can export its data and, on termination, receives a documented window to do so; the retention and deletion policy then applies. Exit terms are not written yet.

**Can we buy it today?**
No. MAREA FLEET is defined and not for sale: no price is approved, no organisation is provisioned and no contract is available.
