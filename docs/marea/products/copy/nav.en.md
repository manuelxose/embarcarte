One application to prepare the passage ashore, follow it on board and record what happened. MAREA NAV does not try to replace your judgement or your equipment: it organises the information you already have or choose to connect, shows where each data point comes from, and makes clear which part depends on a licence, a compatible instrument or a specific AIS source.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **One workspace.** Routes, waypoints, passages, logbook and history stop being scattered across apps and paper.
- **Licensed charts, with no hidden assumptions.** You work with the chart coverage you have licensed; the application neither presumes charts are included nor promises worldwide coverage.
- **Onboard data when it exists.** Instruments, AIS and forecasts appear only when compatible equipment or entitlements are present, always with source and timestamp.
- **Your records stay yours.** Routes, waypoints and logbook entries can be exported, including if you decide not to renew.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA NAV is the navigation workspace of the MAREA family: route planning, licensed charts, device positioning, a digital logbook and onboard data overlays in a common interface for phone, tablet and desktop. It is intended for coastal sailors, weekend cruisers and owners preparing a longer passage, and for those modernising a boat without replacing all the electronics at once.

The application is defined as a navigational aid. It is not an ECDIS system, it does not replace nautical charts or the vessel's required equipment, and it does not replace continuous watchkeeping or good seamanship. Its value is in bringing information together, showing its provenance and making records easier; the course decision and the safety of the vessel always rest with the skipper.

**Product status:** complete definition (DEFINED), not available for purchase. This page describes the intended product; it is not a commercial offer or a promise of availability.

## 8. Features and capabilities

### Core software
- **Unified navigation workspace:** chart, course, position, active route, waypoints and instruments in one working view.
- **Route and passage planning:** create and edit routes, waypoints and saved destinations, with route libraries you can reuse.
- **Digital logbook:** passage records, notes, hours and your own data, with searchable history.
- **Device GNSS positioning:** MAREA NAV uses the position provided by the phone, tablet or computer; it does not include a GNSS receiver of its own.
- **Status alerts:** loss of position, stale data, a disconnected source or missing chart coverage is shown on screen so that missing data is not mistaken for valid data.
- **Export of your own records:** routes, waypoints and logbook entries can be exported in documented formats.
- **Account and multi-device access:** one account lets you work from several devices; how many can be active at the same time depends on the edition.

### Licensed data
- **Nautical charts:** displayed only within the coverage and terms of the licence you have acquired. They are not included by default and access to unlicensed regions is not presumed.
- **Tides and currents:** layers available only with the relevant entitlement and in the areas covered by the source.
- **Weather forecasts:** require a MAREA WEATHER entitlement or an authorised equivalent source; the application does not supply forecasts by itself.
- **Provenance and freshness:** every data layer shows source and timestamp where the source provides them; stale or unavailable states are marked explicitly.

### Connected instruments (optional)
- **Instrument overlays:** wind, depth, heading and speed only with compatible equipment connected and verified in the published compatibility matrix.
- **AIS visualisation:** only with a compatible AIS source; MAREA NAV does not include an AIS receiver. Received targets are shown as traffic information, not as a collision-avoidance system.
- **CPA/TCPA:** decision support when the AIS source and position data are valid; calculation is not guaranteed when information is missing.
- **No steering control:** MAREA NAV does not steer the vessel and does not connect to a steering system to pilot it.

### Optional hardware
- **MAREA HUB:** brings compatible equipment data into one local vessel interface and makes it available to MAREA NAV.
- **MAREA AIS:** receive-only hardware that adds AIS traffic to the navigation workspace when installed and compatible.
- **MAREA DISPLAY:** fixed helm display for consulting navigation without relying on a portable device.

### MAREA WEATHER (optional)
- **Forecast and weather layers:** a separate service, with source and timestamp visible on every data point and coverage subject to each provider's licences.
- **No duplicate billing (planned model):** the design intent is for one MAREA WEATHER entitlement to be shared with the MAREA NAV account; this model is still being defined and is not presented as implemented.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**Preparing a morning departure from home.** You open MAREA NAV on the computer, check the chart coverage for the area, mark two waypoints and save the route. At the home berth the same route is on the tablet; the device GNSS places you alongside and the logbook is ready to record departure. If chart coverage or connectivity fails, the application says so instead of showing invented data.

**A coastal passage with onboard data.** With compatible instruments connected, you see wind and depth over the chart and review the AIS targets received. The course decision remains yours and visual watchkeeping is not delegated to the screen. If AIS reception stops or an instrument disconnects, the application marks that source as unavailable and you continue with the equipment and charts carried on board.

**Reviewing the season at the home berth.** In winter you open the history, go over the recorded passages, correct notes and export your routes and logbook wherever you choose to keep them. This is an archive and planning use, not real-time navigation.

## 13. Compatibility
MAREA NAV compatibility is assessed **per device, per operating-system version and per firmware version**, and is published in a compatibility matrix. There is no universal compatibility: no device is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **Devices:** the application is defined for phone, tablet and desktop; supported operating-system versions will be published with the product.
- **Instruments and gateways:** each device, protocol and message is checked separately. Signal K is not presented as a blanket compatibility guarantee.
- **NMEA 2000:** its status is unresolved in the programme; no NMEA 2000 certification or operational compatibility claim is made.
- **AIS sources:** only data from compatible and verified receivers or sources is displayed; buying MAREA NAV does not imply owning AIS hardware.
- **Charts and data:** compatibility includes coverage and licence terms, not only file format.
- **No control path:** no public steering-control path is offered or implied from MAREA NAV.

Before any planned purchase, a compatibility review of your vessel is the step that determines which configuration is valid and what remains out of scope.

## 14. How it works
1. **Create your account** and choose the edition that matches the way you sail.
2. **Add the chart coverage** you have licensed and check that your sailing area is available.
3. **Plan the passage** with routes, waypoints and saved destinations; the library stays associated with your account.
4. **Connect what you have:** device GNSS always; instruments, AIS or a HUB only when compatible and after the relevant review.
5. **Sail and record:** follow the route, consult data with its source and timestamp, and write the passage into the logbook.
6. **Export and archive** your routes, waypoints and records whenever you want, including at the end of the service relationship.

## 15. Onboarding and installation
MAREA NAV is software: it does not require a physical onboard installation to work with device GNSS. Onboarding consists of creating the account, installing the application, signing in, configuring chart coverage and checking positioning and status alerts.

Integrations that depend on hardware — instruments, AIS, a HUB or a fixed display — require a prior compatibility review and, depending on the configuration, installation and configuration by EMBARCARTE or an installer. No universal installation is promised and no installation duration or price is stated without measurement and quotation. The compatibility review states what can be installed, what needs adaptation and what is out of scope.

## 19. FAQ
**Are nautical charts included?**
No. MAREA NAV includes access to the application and displays charts within the coverage you have licensed; chart data is acquired separately and availability depends on each source and region.

**What hardware do I need to start?**
A phone, tablet or computer with GNSS. No separate GNSS receiver or HUB is required for planning and device positioning. Instruments, AIS and fixed displays are optional.

**Does it work without internet?**
Only in regions where the data licence permits it and with the data downloaded. Offline navigation is not promised in every area or worldwide.

**Is it compatible with my plotter or instruments?**
Compatibility is checked per device, system version and firmware in the published matrix. There is no universal compatibility; the vessel compatibility review determines what can be integrated.

**Do I need MAREA HUB?**
Not for the basic experience with device GNSS. The HUB provides integration of existing equipment and onboard data, and only when the configuration is compatible.

**Do I need an AIS receiver?**
Only if you want to see AIS traffic. MAREA NAV does not include AIS hardware; visualisation requires a compatible, installed AIS source.

**What happens if I cancel the subscription?**
You stop renewing and access to subscription functions ends at the end of the period. Your routes, waypoints and logbook remain exportable; charts and third-party data may stop being available.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes: routes, waypoints, passages and your own records can be exported in documented formats. Licensed third-party data may be subject to export restrictions.

**Is it an ECDIS?**
No. MAREA NAV is a navigational aid and is not an ECDIS system or presented as equivalent. It does not replace nautical charts or required equipment.

**What happens if position or connectivity is lost?**
The application shows the data state and alerts you when position or a source is unavailable. Navigation remains the skipper's responsibility, using the charts and equipment carried on board.
