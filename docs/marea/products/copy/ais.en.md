## 4. Hero supporting statement
MAREA AIS is the receive-only AIS receiver of the MAREA family: it shows the AIS targets received and makes them available to navigation and compatible equipment. It does not transmit the boat's position, it is not a transponder, and it does not replace watchkeeping or existing equipment.

## 5. Main value proposition
MAREA AIS adds traffic awareness to a boat that already sails with its own equipment: it receives AIS transmissions from vessels that send them and presents them as traffic information to support decision-making. It is **receive-only**: it does not transmit, does not respond, does not identify the boat to others and is not a collision-avoidance system. Its value is in seeing what equipment and eyesight alone do not always show, while always knowing that not every vessel transmits AIS.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **Receive-only AIS traffic awareness.** Receives and displays the AIS targets arriving over radio, with identity and movement when the data is valid.
- **Integrates with what is compatible.** Can publish received data to MAREA NAV, MAREA HUB, MAREA DISPLAY or other compatible systems, according to the published matrix.
- **Decision-support information.** Helps interpret received traffic; it does not decide for you and does not avoid collisions.
- **Declared limits.** The page and documentation explain what an AIS receiver cannot do, so that the reassurance of a screen is not mistaken for safety.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA AIS is a receive-only AIS receiver for recreational vessels that want awareness of nearby traffic without installing a transponder. It receives transmissions from vessels that transmit AIS and turns them into consultable targets: identity, position, course and movement when the received messages are valid. The data is shown in the application or compatible equipment, and can be integrated with MAREA NAV and MAREA HUB when the compatibility matrix confirms it.

The product is **receive-only**. It does not transmit the boat's position, it is not a transponder, it does not include DSC or distress functions and it is not a collision-avoidance system. It does not replace continuous watchkeeping, radar, required equipment or nautical charts. Reception depends on the antenna, splitter, cabling, power and installation; it is not guaranteed in any condition.

## 8. Features and capabilities

### Reception and software
- **Receive-only AIS reception:** receives AIS transmissions from vessels that send them and presents them as traffic targets.
- **Identification and tracking:** shows the identity and movement of received targets when the data is valid and up to date.
- **Traffic list:** lets you consult and sort received targets, with status and data-age markers.
- **Own-position data:** CPA/TCPA estimation needs the boat's own position and heading; if these are missing or invalid, the calculation is not presented as reliable.
- **Reception status:** shows whether the receiver is receiving, has lost the source, or has a stale target.
- **Configurable alerts:** traffic and stale-data notices; they are not safety alarms and do not replace watchkeeping.
- **Diagnostics:** power, connection and reception status for installation and maintenance.
- **Updates:** the firmware update policy will be published with the product and confirmed by the seller before sale; the purchase will include a defined support period.
- **No transmission:** there is no transmit path, no transponder function is provided and the boat does not announce itself over AIS.

### Data and entitlements
- **AIS-derived data:** the history, retention or export of AIS data may be subject to data rights and is not presumed included by default.
- **No charts or forecasts included:** MAREA AIS does not include chart data, forecasts, tides or currents; those layers depend on their own licences and on the product that displays them.
- **Provenance and timestamp:** each target shows the time of the last received data where the source provides it; an out-of-date target is marked as such.

### Connected equipment (optional)
- **MAREA NAV:** displays received targets in the navigation workspace when the integration is verified.
- **MAREA HUB:** managed integration in MAREA AIS Connected and documented local output in MAREA AIS Receiver, always per the matrix and verified design.
- **MAREA DISPLAY:** lets the traffic be consulted on a fixed helm display.
- **Local outputs:** the documented output methods are published per version; no output is announced that has not been verified.

### Hardware and installation
- **Dedicated VHF antenna:** required for reception; it is not presumed included or free.
- **VHF splitter:** may be required to share the antenna with the VHF equipment; it is not presumed included or free.
- **Additional cabling, mast coax, connectors and mounting items not included:** quoted according to the installation; the basic power lead and mounting bracket are part of the intended package.
- **Technical installation:** antenna and radio work requires specialist installation; it is not presented as included in the product.

### MAREA WEATHER (optional)
- **Separate service:** MAREA WEATHER is a licensed forecast service and is not part of MAREA AIS. The planned shared entitlement between MAREA NAV and MAREA WEATHER neither includes nor changes the purchase of MAREA AIS.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**A coastal crossing with traffic.** You sail with MAREA NAV and MAREA AIS integrated; the screen shows the AIS targets received, with identity and movement when the data is valid. You keep looking outside, listening on VHF and checking the course; the screen adds information, it does not replace watchkeeping. If a target stops updating, it is marked as stale instead of continuing to be presented as current.

**A night approach to harbour.** AIS helps you place the vessels that transmit near the entrance, but not every boat transmits AIS: small sailboats, vessels without equipment and obstacles do not appear. The approach is made with the chart, eyesight, equipment and normal caution; the receiver is not a collision-avoidance system.

**A refit with an installer.** During a refit the antenna location, possible use of a splitter, cabling and power are reviewed. The installer checks reception and documents the installation; duration and price are quoted after that review, not announced in advance. The antenna, splitter and cabling are not presumed included.

## 19. FAQ
**Does MAREA AIS transmit my boat's position?**
No. It is a receive-only receiver: it does not transmit, does not respond and does not announce the boat to others. It is not a transponder.

**Is it a collision-avoidance system?**
No. It shows received AIS traffic as supporting information; it does not avoid collisions, does not calculate a safe manoeuvre and does not replace watchkeeping or required equipment.

**Does it show every boat around me?**
No. Only vessels that transmit AIS and whose signal is received appear; many boats do not transmit AIS and others may not be received because of antenna, distance, power or installation conditions.

**Do I need MAREA NAV or MAREA HUB to use it?**
Not for reception. Integration with MAREA NAV, MAREA HUB or MAREA DISPLAY depends on the matrix and version; without a compatible system, the receiver remains a receive-only receiver.

**What antenna do I need?**
It depends on the vessel and on whether the VHF antenna is shared through a splitter. The installation review determines the solution; the antenna and splitter are not presumed included.

**Are antenna, splitter, cables and installation included?**
The intended package includes the receiver, a power lead, a quick-start guide and a mounting bracket. It does not include the AIS VHF antenna, splitter, mast coax or specialist installation; those items are quoted separately.

**Is a reception range guaranteed?**
No. Reception depends on the antenna, cabling, power, installation and radio conditions; no guaranteed range is announced.

**Does it work without internet?**
Reception is local and does not need internet to receive. Integration with other products may depend on the local network and configuration; internet connectivity is not required for listening.

**Can I store and export traffic history?**
History and export of AIS-derived data are subject to applicable data rights and are not included by default; availability is published per version and configuration.

**What happens if a target stops updating?**
It is marked as stale or unavailable and stops being presented as current. The application does not invent a position or speed it has not received.

**What is the NMEA 2000 status?**
No NMEA 2000 certification or operational compatibility claim is made; its status is unresolved in the programme.
