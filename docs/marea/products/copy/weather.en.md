# MAREA WEATHER - Product page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA WEATHER: marine forecasts with source, time and declared coverage

## 2. Meta description
MAREA WEATHER brings wind, gusts, waves, currents and atmospheric conditions together with the source and time of every value. It is a navigational aid: forecasts are not guarantees and the decision stays with the skipper.

## 3. Hero headline
Know what the forecast says, where it comes from and how old it is.

## 4. Hero supporting statement
Wind, gusts, waves, currents, tides and atmospheric conditions in one place, with the model, the model run time, the valid time, the receipt time and the age of every value in view. No accuracy promises, and no hiding when a value is missing, stale or outside coverage.

## 5. Main value proposition
Most forecast applications show a number. MAREA WEATHER also shows where that number comes from, what moment it is valid for, when it was received and how old it is. That difference is what lets a skipper decide on evidence: when two models disagree, the disagreement is visible; when a value is old, it is labelled as old; when an area is not covered, it says so; and when a provider stops responding, the surface admits it instead of showing a frozen forecast as if it were current.

MAREA WEATHER is defined as a weather and oceanographic information aid. It is not a meteorological guarantee, it is not an official warning authority, and it does not replace the skipper's judgement or the decision to stay in port.

**Product status:** complete definition, not available for purchase. This page describes the intended product; it is not a commercial offer or a promise of availability.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **Visible provenance.** Every value carries provider, product, model, model run time, valid time, receipt time, age, units, method, licence and limitations.
- **Clear separation of data types.** A forecast is never shown as an observation; a historical value is never shown as current; a MAREA computation is labelled as a computation; customer-entered data is identified as such; and an unknown origin is never presented as a forecast.
- **Honest freshness.** Current, aging, stale, expired, unavailable and out-of-coverage states are shown with their reason, so "no data" is never mistaken for "all normal".
- **Transparent comparison.** In the Pro edition, several models can be read side by side, each with its own identity and time, and with the spread shown as a spread. A consensus is never fabricated and a "best model" is never chosen.
- **Declared coverage.** Each source states which area it covers, at what spatial and temporal resolution and out to what horizon. Anything outside it is declared out of coverage.
- **One account and one entitlement.** The service can be bought standalone and, where the data licence allows it, can be included as equivalent read access in the navigation subscription without charging for the same access twice.
- **Bilingual marine terminology.** The same facts in Spanish and in English, with marine terms defined consistently.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA WEATHER is the marine weather and oceanographic information product of the MAREA family. It brings together wind and gust forecasts, wave height, direction and period, swell where the licensed source publishes it, currents, tides where their licence permits, sea surface temperature where licensed and atmospheric conditions, always with the provenance and time of every value.

The product is organised around declared sailing areas and saved marine locations. For each one the customer can read which variables are available, from which source, at what declared resolution and out to what horizon, as well as which variables are unavailable and why. The timeline lets the customer move through each source's horizon; model comparison, available in Pro, shows whether the sources agree or disagree.

MAREA WEATHER focuses on a declared pilot geography: Galicia, including the Rías Baixas, the adjacent Atlantic waters of the Iberian northwest and Atlantic Portugal. That is a planning scope, not a granted coverage: actual coverage depends on licensed providers and is declared source by source.

**What MAREA WEATHER is not:** it is not an official meteorological warning service, it does not replace official warnings, it is not an accuracy guarantee, it does not include nautical charts, it does not execute routes or steer the vessel, and it is not a distress service.

## 8. Features and capabilities

**Home screen.** Answers the practical question: what is the weather doing where I sail, how old is that value and where did it come from, before showing any interpretation.

**Selectable sailing areas.** The customer chooses among declared areas, and the service explains what each covers, at what resolution and horizon, and what it does not cover.

**Saved marine locations.** Harbour entrances, anchorages, clubs and points of interest with their variable availability and per-location freshness, exportable by the customer.

**Wind and gusts.** Sustained wind speed and true direction, with gusts shown separately and never conflated with sustained wind.

**Waves.** Significant wave height, direction and period, with the direction convention and the period definition stated on screen.

**Swell.** Only where the licensed source publishes a swell partition. If it does not, the variable is declared unavailable with a reason and is never inferred from total sea state.

**Currents.** Surface current speed and direction, labelled forecast or observation according to the source.

**Tides.** Tide height with station or model, datum, prediction basis, source, valid time and age. It is not presented as an official tide table without a written right to do so.

**Sea surface temperature.** Where its licence permits, labelled forecast or observation as appropriate.

**Atmospheric conditions.** Air temperature, pressure at mean sea level, cloud cover and precipitation, each with units, source and time.

**Map layers.** Only layers whose licence exists. An unlicensed layer is not shown and the reason is explained; it is never taken from an unauthorised service.

**Timelines.** A time axis per variable aligned on valid time, with model run time and receipt time available at each step and the horizon boundary marked.

**Model comparison (Pro).** Several models side by side, each with its provenance and time, and with the spread shown as a spread. No invented consensus, no mean presented as a forecast and no "best model" ranking.

**Source identification and attribution.** Provider, product, model and dataset version for every value, plus a provenance page listing every source and its licence.

**Model run, valid and receipt timestamps.** All three are shown separately and never substituted for one another.

**Horizon, coverage and resolution.** Per source and per variable, with the boundary marked and no point-precision promise.

**Data quality and status.** Freshness states and provider quality flags where they exist, with nothing invented.

**Missing data and stale forecasts.** A missing value is shown as missing and never as zero. A stale, expired or unavailable forecast is labelled and never presented as current.

**Uncertainty where the provider publishes it.** Shown as the provider publishes it, with its meaning; if no such product exists, the field states that uncertainty is not provided.

**Historical comparison (Pro, where licensed).** Comparison with past forecasts or reanalysis only where an archive right exists, stating provider, period and method.

**Route weather context (Pro).** Values sampled along a route, labelled as a MAREA computation and as advisory, with the method and the contributing models named.

**Passage planning and weather windows (Pro).** Organising departure, route context and a computed window against thresholds declared by the customer. Always advisory, never a guarantee.

**Configurable notifications (Pro).** Rules on locations and windows, with delivery limits and provider dependence explained. They are not official warnings and not a guaranteed immediate alert.

**Account and entitlements.** One account with the entitlement record, saved areas and locations, language preference, the provenance record and export and deletion controls.

**MAREA NAV integration.** Read-only weather context for the navigation workspace. MAREA WEATHER does not include charts, a logbook or navigation execution.

**Authorised caching and offline presentation.** Only where the source licence permits it. An offline surface states the last receipt time and that the value is not current.

**Usage limits.** The customer's plan limits and the provider call budget are declared; when the budget is exhausted only cached values are served, with their age.

**Provider outage handling.** The service shows an unavailable state, the last successful receipt, the affected provider and keeps the attribution.

**Support.** Questions about provenance and freshness, coverage, entitlements and billing, and export of the provenance record of a decision already taken. Support is not an interpretation guarantee and not an emergency channel.

**Renewal and cancellation.** Renewal date and terms before any charge, in-product cancellation, prorated upgrade from Standard to Pro and export and retention rules.

**Bilingual marine terminology.** The same facts in Spanish and English, with a glossary defining swell, gust, significant wave height, tide datum and current.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
- **Deciding on a sailing day in the Rías.** The skipper opens the declared area, reviews wind and gusts with their valid time, checks how old the value is and decides whether to leave in the morning or wait.
- **Preparing a short coastal passage on the Atlantic coast.** Two models are compared in Pro, the disagreement in wind is identified, and an advisory window is chosen inside the thresholds the customer declared.
- **Checking sea state before a sports fishing trip.** Wave height, direction and period with the declared resolution of the source, and an explicit check of whether swell is available at all.
- **Checking a current before entering a harbour.** Surface current labelled forecast or observation according to the source, with its time.
- **Looking up the tide at a specific point.** Tide height with station or model, datum and prediction basis, and without presenting it as an official table.
- **Organising a sailing school's week.** Several saved locations with per-location variable availability and freshness, exportable.
- **Facing a provider outage.** The screen shows the unavailable state and the last successful receipt instead of a frozen forecast.

## 14. How it works
1. The customer chooses a declared sailing area and saves marine locations.
2. The service requests the licensed sources for that area and moment, and records the model run, the validity of the value and the receipt time.
3. Every value is presented with its full provenance, its units and its age, and with the freshness state that applies to it.
4. If a variable is not covered, is out of coverage or has no licence, it is declared unavailable with the reason.
5. In Pro, models are shown side by side with their spread, and route context, planning and windows are computed and labelled as advisory.
6. If a provider stops responding or the call budget is exhausted, the service says so and does not invent a forecast.

## 19. FAQ
**Where does the data come from?** From licensed providers identified on the page and in every value. No commercial rights are granted today, so no source is announced as available for sale.
**How old is this forecast?** Every value shows the model run time, the valid time, the receipt time and the age, and is labelled if it is aging, stale or expired.
**Why do two models disagree?** Because they are different models. The product shows the disagreement and does not turn it into an invented consensus.
**What happens if a provider fails?** An unavailable state is shown with the last successful receipt and the attribution is kept.
**Do you show official warnings?** No. MAREA WEATHER is not an official warning authority and does not publish official warnings.
**Does it work offline?** Only if the source licence permits it. If not, the offline surface states that the value is not current.
**Which areas are covered?** The declared pilot geography, with each source's coverage and resolution shown. Anything else is out of coverage.
**Is it a meteorological guarantee?** No. Forecasts are probabilistic and may differ from actual local conditions.
**Is it certified?** No certification of this product exists.
**Do MAREA WEATHER and MAREA NAV cost twice?** No. There is a single entitlement record and the same access is not charged twice.
**Can I export my data?** Saved areas and locations and preferences are the customer's and can be exported within the retention policy.
**What happens if I cancel?** Access ends at the end of the period, the renewal date is shown before charging, and the customer's own records follow the retention and export policy.
**Can I get a forecast for my exact position?** Only within the declared grid and resolution; no point precision finer than the source is promised.
