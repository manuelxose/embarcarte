## 3. Hero headline
Know what your boat measures. And what it does not.

## 4. Hero supporting statement
MAREA SENSE is a family of onboard measurement modules: wind, power, bilge, tanks and environment. Each module declares what it measures, over what range, with what calibration and with what limits. Data is shown in a compatible HUB or another tested consumer; receiving alerts ashore requires MAREA WATCH, network coverage and an active subscription. A module on its own has no connectivity.

## 5. Main value proposition
Measuring is not the same as showing a number. It means knowing where the value comes from, when it was updated, whether it is calibrated and whether it is still inside its range. MAREA SENSE organises that information module by module: start with the measurement you are missing, install and calibrate it with a documented procedure, and see every value with its source and its state. What has not been verified is shown as not verified; a value that is out of range or uncalibrated is not presented as a reliable measurement. We do not promise accuracy we have not measured, or an alarm that is not certified.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **Transparency in every reading.** Source, units, time, quality state and calibration state travel with the value; a stale or invalid reading is shown as such.
- **Modular entry.** Start with the one module you actually need, without replacing the equipment that already works at the helm.
- **One data language.** With a compatible HUB, measurements come together in the same local layer and reach MAREA NAV, MAREA DISPLAY and MAREA WATCH.
- **Remote alerts only where they belong.** Remote monitoring is a MAREA WATCH function with its own hardware, coverage and subscription; no SENSE module sends alerts by itself.
- **A history the boat can use.** Installation and calibration records are documented, which helps with maintenance and with a future sale of the vessel.
- **No cloud dependency for measuring.** Local measurement and the calibration record do not depend on an internet connection or a subscription.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA SENSE is the onboard measurement and monitoring family of MAREA. It consists of five separately purchased modules — Wind, Power, Bilge, Tanks and Environment — and three preconfigured sets: MAREA SENSE Essentials, MAREA SENSE Sail and MAREA SENSE Systems.

Each module is intended for one specific measurement. Wind measures apparent wind at the masthead and, only with valid vessel heading and speed, true wind. Power measures voltage and charge or discharge current of the battery or system. Bilge reports water presence in a bilge compartment as a state, not as a continuous level. Tanks calculates tank contents from a compatible sender and the tank geometry. Environment measures temperature, relative humidity and barometric pressure. None of them is required equipment, a certified alarm or a navigation instrument.

## 8. Features and capabilities

### Measurement modules
- **MAREA SENSE Wind.** Apparent wind at the masthead: speed and angle, aligned to the vessel centreline. True wind is shown only when valid heading and speed are available; if they are missing, it is shown as unavailable. Requires alignment calibration and speed verification.
- **MAREA SENSE Power.** Battery or system voltage and charge or discharge current of the monitored circuit, with derived power and energy counters. It is not a battery-management system and does not present state of charge as a reliable measurement.
- **MAREA SENSE Bilge.** Water presence in a bilge compartment through a switch or contact: dry, water detected, fault or unknown. It is not a continuous level and does not replace the vessel's required bilge-alarm arrangement.
- **MAREA SENSE Tanks.** Contents of a fresh-water, fuel or waste tank from a compatible sender and the tank capacity table. Accuracy depends on the sender and the geometry; it is not a fuel-management system or a legal-metrology instrument.
- **MAREA SENSE Environment.** Temperature, relative humidity and barometric pressure in a protected compartment, with derived dew point. It is not a weather forecast or a fire, gas or carbon-monoxide detector.

### What accompanies every reading
- **Source and units.** Every value declares its module, its source, the quantity and the published unit.
- **Time and freshness.** The value carries its timestamp and its age; when updates stop, it becomes stale and then unavailable.
- **Quality state.** Valid, invalid, unknown, out of range, stale or fault are displayed explicitly.
- **Calibration state.** A module that is uncalibrated, has an overdue calibration or has changed configuration does not present its number as a reliable measurement.
- **Adapter mode distinguished.** When a module consumes another manufacturer's data, the external source is identified and the reading is marked as not verified by MAREA.

### Integration and dependencies
- **Compatible HUB.** The intended path for bringing measurements together and delivering them to MAREA NAV, MAREA DISPLAY and MAREA WATCH. The HUB does not measure: it receives, publishes and displays what the modules measure.
- **WATCH.** The separate product that enables alerts away from the boat; it requires connectivity and a subscription, and delivery depends on power, network coverage and the notification provider.
- **Local display.** Optional: a MAREA DISPLAY through the HUB, or a third-party display tested in the compatibility matrix. A display cannot make an uncalibrated reading valid.
- **Tested third-party consumer.** Only where the compatibility matrix records the device, firmware version and data direction.

### What the family does not include
- It does not include a HUB, WATCH, DISPLAY or NAV.
- It does not include connectivity, a SIM card, a cloud service or remote alerts.
- It does not include charts, weather forecasts or navigation functions.
- It does not include a certified alarm or replace the vessel's required equipment.
- It does not include steering, an autopilot or any control output.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**Checking the battery before the season.** You install the Power module with its calibration and see the voltage and current of the monitored circuit in the HUB. If the value has not been updated for a while or the sensor stops responding, you see it marked stale or faulty instead of accepting a reassuring number.

**A bilge that worries you when you are away.** The Bilge module monitors water presence in the chosen compartment. If you also want to know ashore, MAREA WATCH sends the alert when the condition is met, within its limits: it depends on power, network coverage and provider delivery, and it is not a distress service or a certified alarm.

**Tanks mid-passage.** With the sender and the tank table configured, you see each tank's contents with its state. The percentage is a conversion from the sender and the geometry: if a sender sticks at a plausible value, the screen cannot know by itself, and the documentation says so.

**Sailing with wind data in order.** With a compatible HUB and a MAREA DISPLAY or NAV, apparent wind appears with its time and state. Course decisions and navigation remain the skipper's; MAREA SENSE does not navigate or steer the boat.

**Maintenance and handover.** When a sensor is replaced, the replacement procedure states whether re-calibration is required and leaves a new record. The installation and calibration records stay with the vessel, which helps with maintenance and with selling to a new owner.

## 13. Compatibility
MAREA SENSE compatibility is assessed **per module, per firmware version, per consumer and per data direction**, and published in a matrix. There is no universal compatibility: no device is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **Modules and firmware:** each firmware version is tested with each documented consumer; an update may change compatibility and this is stated.
- **HUB:** the intended path for integration with MAREA NAV, MAREA DISPLAY and MAREA WATCH; the local data contract includes units, quality, calibration and freshness.
- **NMEA 0183:** where a module acts as an adapter, it reads the documented sentences listed in the matrix; reading sentences is not conformity with the NMEA 0183 standard and is not certification.
- **NMEA 2000:** not implemented or certified in the programme; no operational NMEA 2000 compatibility is announced.
- **Signal K:** not presented as a blanket compatibility guarantee; it is checked per source, version, path and direction.
- **Third-party consumer:** only where the matrix records the device, version and direction; otherwise the outcome is an assessment, not a promise.
- **No control path:** no SENSE module publishes steering commands or connects to a steering system.

Before any planned purchase, a compatibility review of your vessel determines which configuration is valid and what remains out of scope.

## 14. How it works
1. **Choose the measurement you are missing.** Wind, power, bilge, tanks or environment; the sets combine modules already described.
2. **Review compatibility.** The compartment or tank, existing sender or sensor, intended consumer and firmware version are checked.
3. **Install.** A documented low-risk configuration may be installed by the owner; electrical, tank, masthead or engine-space work requires professional installation.
4. **Calibrate.** Each module has its procedure and reference class; calibration produces a dated record with the method, the points and the result.
5. **Verify.** Valid, invalid, stale and fault states are demonstrated so that a missing value is not mistaken for a normal one.
6. **Use and expand.** See measurements in the HUB and, if needed, in NAV, DISPLAY or WATCH; add modules or replace a sensor with its re-calibration.

## 15. Onboarding and installation
Commissioning is a documented sequence, not a manual and a hope. The boat, compartment or tank and intended consumer are identified; the documented items are mounted and connected; power, identification and communication are checked; calibration is performed against the applicable reference; installation and calibration records are written; and the owner or installer is shown how invalid, stale and fault states look.

Professional installation is intended for every configuration outside the documented low-risk set: work on the DC distribution, battery shunt, tank senders, masthead sensors and cabling, and any installation in fuel or engine spaces. Replacing a module with an equivalent item in a documented configuration may be an owner replacement where the calibration can be repeated and recorded.

No installation or calibration duration and no installation price is announced without measurement and quotation. The compatibility review states what can be installed, what needs adaptation and what is out of scope.

## 19. FAQ
**What does each module measure?**
Wind measures apparent wind (true wind only with valid heading and speed); Power measures battery or system voltage and current; Bilge reports water presence in a bilge compartment; Tanks reports tank contents from a sender; Environment measures temperature, humidity and pressure in a compartment.

**Do I need a HUB?**
To see measurements in MAREA NAV, MAREA DISPLAY or MAREA WATCH, and to bring them together in one local layer, yes. A module may feed a tested third-party consumer only if the compatibility matrix records it; a module without a consumer shows no data.

**Do I need MAREA WATCH to receive alerts?**
Yes. A SENSE module has no connectivity and sends no alerts. WATCH is a separate product that requires connectivity and a subscription, and delivery depends on power, network coverage and the notification provider.

**What accuracy does it have?**
No accuracy figure is announced: measurement accuracy has not been measured. Each module declares its intended range and calibration requirement, and an uncalibrated, out-of-range or stale reading is not presented as reliable.

**Does it need calibration?**
Yes. Each module has its calibration procedure and reference class, and calibration leaves a dated record. Replacing a sensor or sender requires re-calibration according to the module procedure.

**Can I install it myself?**
Only in a documented, confirmed low-risk configuration, and only if you can perform and record the calibration. Electrical, battery, tank, masthead or engine-space work requires professional installation.

**What happens if a sensor fails?**
The value becomes fault, stale or unavailable with its age; an old number is never kept as if it were current. Some single-point failures, such as a bilge switch stuck dry, cannot be detected without redundancy, and the documentation explains this.

**Is it a certified alarm?**
No. MAREA SENSE is not a certified alarm and does not replace the vessel's required equipment, including any applicable bilge-alarm arrangement.

**Is it NMEA 2000 compatible?**
No. NMEA 2000 is not implemented or certified in the programme; no operational compatibility with that standard is announced.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes: local measurement and the calibration record do not depend on a connection. What does not work without coverage is the remote WATCH alert, which also requires a subscription.

**Can I sell the boat with the installation?**
Yes, through a documented transfer of the installation and calibration records. Warranty does not reset with a change of owner, and support terms transfer only as published.

**What does it cost?**
No price is published: the product is not for sale and this text is not an offer. Any programme figure is an internal hypothesis pending approval and is not presented as a purchase price.
