# MAREA WATCH — Product page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA WATCH: remote boat monitoring with conditional alerts

## 2. Meta description
MAREA WATCH watches your vessel's position, battery and bilge and alerts the people you authorise. Conditional awareness: it depends on power, network coverage and the notification provider. It is not a certified distress service.

## 3. Hero headline
Know what is happening on board when you are not there, without promising what the network cannot deliver.

## 4. Hero supporting statement
MAREA WATCH is a monitoring device with a service subscription: it measures the compatible inputs you declare, evaluates your rules and geofence, and alerts the people you authorise when the vessel is left unattended. Every value is shown with its time and age, "accepted by the provider" is kept distinct from "delivered" and from "confirmed by a person", and the product explains exactly what happens when connectivity is lost.

## 5. Main value proposition
MAREA WATCH is intended for a boat that sits unattended in a marina, mooring or yard for days or weeks. It does not turn monitoring into a safety promise: it organises what can actually be measured —position, battery voltage, water in the bilge and connection health— publishes it with its source and time, and alerts when a condition crosses the threshold you set. When there is no data, it says so. When there is a connectivity gap, it shows how long it lasted. And when an alert is delivered, it never claims that someone has seen it: only a person can confirm that.

## 6. Principal customer benefits
- **You know what is watched and what is not.** The monitored inputs are published before purchase and confirmed at installation. Anything outside that list is never presented as watched.
- **Every value carries its time and state.** An old reading is shown as the last known value with its age, never as if it were current.
- **Gaps are visible, not hidden.** If the boat stops reporting, the application shows since when, what was missed and what was recovered when connectivity returned.
- **Alerts with an evidence trail.** It distinguishes whether the provider accepted the alert, whether delivery has been confirmed and whether a person has explicitly acknowledged it.
- **You decide who sees the vessel.** The owner invites people by role and can revoke access at any time; position is not public by default.
- **The boat keeps working without a subscription.** Local monitoring and local device alerts do not depend on payment; without a subscription the remote service stops, not the local function.

## 7. Product overview
MAREA WATCH is the remote-monitoring offer of the MAREA family for recreational vessels left unattended in a marina, mooring or yard. It combines three things that must be understood separately: a **physical device** installed on board that measures the compatible inputs; **software** for the account, vessel registration, device identity, rules, geofence, history, permissions and audit; and a **service subscription** that pays for the contracted connectivity, the service that receives and stores the data, rule evaluation and alert delivery through external providers. None of the three layers includes the others.

The product is defined to answer one concrete question —"what is happening on the boat, and since when have I not known?"— without exaggerating the answer. It is not a distress service, not a security system, does not prevent theft or sinking and does not allow remote steering of the vessel. It is conditional awareness: it depends on the device's power, on network coverage at the berth and on the notification provider delivering the alert to the right person.

**Product status:** complete definition (DEFINED), not available for purchase. This page describes the intended product; it is not a commercial offer, it contains no prices and it makes no promise of availability.

## 8. Features and capabilities

### Device and physical layer
- **Position monitoring:** device GNSS position with fix quality and age; if the signal is lost, position becomes "unknown" and the geofence is suspended — no position is invented.
- **Battery monitoring:** battery voltage or state where a compatible input exists, with owner-defined thresholds and history.
- **Bilge monitoring:** water presence or level where a compatible input exists; an unpowered or silent sensor is shown as unavailable, never as a dry bilge.
- **Temperature context monitoring:** on-board temperature where a compatible input exists, useful for cold, heat and condensation; shown with source and age.
- **Additional compatible inputs:** only those published as compatible for the purchased configuration; each input states units, intended range and installation requirements. The essential set is part of Core; additional inputs are offered in the Plus edition under the published compatible list.
- **Power and backup energy:** the device is powered from the vessel and, where the final design includes a backup source, its duration will be published only once measured. Power loss is reported where the device can report it.
- **Professional installation:** offered for every configuration and required outside the documented low-risk self-install set; includes vessel and coverage assessment, a written proposal, installation, commissioning, function checks and handover with training.

### Account and monitoring software
- **Vessel registration and account linking:** one profile per vessel, a device identity and a verified owner; an unrelated account cannot claim an already-bound device.
- **Geofence:** one or more owner-defined zones with hysteresis to avoid alert chains; when position is invalid, the geofence does not assert that the vessel is inside.
- **Alerts and rules:** the essential alert set with published thresholds in Core (geofence, bilge, battery, power loss and connectivity loss) and additional configurable rules in the Plus edition, with a plain-language preview of when they would fire.
- **History:** values, state changes, alerts, deliveries, acknowledgements, escalations and connectivity gaps with their times; exportable by the owner.
- **Status dashboard:** each input with value, unit, source, age and freshness state; connection status, last contact, open alerts and last-known-good values.
- **Connectivity status and last-known-good:** the application shows how long there has been no contact and presents the last good value with its age; "no data" is a visible state, not a green light.
- **Local buffering and replay:** when there is no uplink, the device stores what it can locally with its own clock and replays it on reconnection, marked "recorded while offline".
- **Users and permissions:** owner, family/crew and time-limited, audited technician/installer access in both editions; in the Plus edition, marina service and professional fleet are added if validated. Each role has published and revocable permissions.
- **Privacy and audit:** minimisation of the data that leaves the vessel, consent, access and role-change logging, export and deletion under the published policy.
- **Updates, replacement and end of life:** signed updates with rollback, device replacement with revocation of the old unit, ownership transfer and documented decommissioning.

### Service subscription
- **Remote service:** reception and storage of the authorised subset, service-side rule evaluation, alert delivery, escalation, the history window and the included user seats.
- **Transparent renewal:** renewal date, term and conditions before charge; a documented grace period if payment fails.
- **Data plan and SIM/roaming administration:** visible consumption, what happens when the allowance is exhausted, roaming territories and costs before travel. No unlimited or worldwide coverage is promised.
- **Cancellation and account deletion:** distinct, explicit actions, with export offered before the remote service stops retaining data.

### What it does not do
- It is not a distress, rescue, SOLAS or GMDSS service.
- It does not prevent theft, collision, sinking or damage; it is not a security system.
- It does not steer the vessel and offers no remote rudder, heading or engine command of any kind.
- It does not replace watchkeeping, the vessel's required equipment or the skipper's decision.

## 9. Realistic usage scenarios
**A week away with the boat in its berth.** The owner installs the device in a documented configuration, registers the vessel and defines two rules: battery below a threshold for a few minutes, and water in the bilge. During the week the application shows the last known value of each input with its time. One day data transfer stops because of a berth outage; the application shows the time of last contact and, when connectivity returns, the duration of the gap and the values recorded in the meantime.

**An alert that is not confused with a confirmation.** Battery voltage drops during the night and an alert is created. The provider accepts it; the application shows "accepted", not "delivered". When the provider's confirmation arrives it becomes "delivered". In the morning the owner acknowledges it in the application, and that act is recorded with their name and time. If they had not acknowledged it, the alert would have escalated to the next person in the defined order.

**Sharing with the people who need it.** The owner invites their family with a read-only role and gives the trusted technician temporary access to review the battery history. In the Plus edition, if validated, they add the marina service as a weekend escalation contact. At the end of the season they revoke the technician's access and check the access record to confirm the vessel is no longer visible to them.

**Before selling the boat.** The owner starts the transfer, the buyer completes the binding to their account and the previous access is revoked. The subscription, the warranty and what happens to the history are explained in the terms that will be published before sale; there is no promise that the complete history stays available.

## 13. Compatibility
MAREA WATCH compatibility is published **per input, per sensor model where applicable, per firmware version, per unit and per data direction**. There is no universal compatibility: no sensor or device is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **Monitored inputs:** only those in the published documentation for the purchased configuration, with their intended range and calibration requirement where applicable.
- **MAREA SENSE sensors:** only the modules and versions confirmed by the compatibility matrix; SENSE provides no connectivity or remote delivery by itself.
- **MAREA HUB:** a distinct on-board local gateway. WATCH does not include it, does not require it and does not publish its data ashore without a specific, consented service.
- **MAREA AIS:** the defined WATCH product does not display AIS traffic. Any future traffic view would require its own receive-only disclaimer and data-rights review.
- **No control path:** no remote command or steering path to the vessel exists or is planned from WATCH.

Before any planned purchase, the vessel and coverage assessment determines which configuration is valid and what remains out of scope.

## 14. How it works
1. **The device is purchased and the edition is chosen** to match what you want to watch; hardware and subscription are separate things and are explained separately.
2. **Install it, or have it installed professionally,** in a documented configuration; the prior assessment confirms which inputs are possible.
3. **Commission and bind:** the device receives its identity, the vessel is registered and ownership is verified before the account is linked.
4. **Define the people:** the owner invites family, crew, technician or marina service by role, according to the edition and with scoped permissions.
5. **Configure rules and geofence:** with a preview of when they would fire and the freshness state that will apply to each input.
6. **Test the alerts:** one end-to-end test verifies the path from observation to a person's acknowledgement and records every step.
7. **Use and review:** the application shows state, connectivity gaps, last-known-good values and history; the owner can export their data and revoke access at any time.

## 19. FAQ
**What exactly does MAREA WATCH monitor?**
The monitored inputs listed in the published documentation for your configuration: in the essential configuration, position, battery/DC state with shore-power context, bilge, temperature and connection status. Nothing outside that list is presented as watched.

**Does it guarantee that I will receive the alert?**
No. The alert depends on device power, network coverage and delivery by the notification provider. The application distinguishes "accepted by the provider", "delivered" and "confirmed by a person", and never claims more than it can prove.

**What happens if the marina network fails?**
The device stores what it can locally with its own clock and replays it when connectivity returns. The application shows last contact, the gap duration and the values recorded in the meantime, marked "recorded while offline".

**What happens if the power fails?**
The device reports power loss where it can. If the final design includes backup energy, its duration will be published only after measurement; no duration is promised today.

**Does it work anywhere in the world?**
No. There is no unlimited or worldwide coverage promise. Coverage and terms depend on the contracted connectivity service, which does not exist yet; roaming will be explained before travel.

**Does it prevent theft or sinking?**
No. MAREA WATCH reports conditions within its compatible inputs; it does not prevent, deter or recover anything. It is not a security system and not a certified distress service.

**Can it steer the boat?**
No. WATCH has no remote rudder, heading or engine command, and no steering-control path is offered.

**Who can see my vessel?**
Only the people the owner authorises, by role and with scoped permissions. Position is not public by default, there are no public links in the defined product, and the owner can revoke access at any time; revocation takes effect immediately and is recorded.

**What does the subscription include?**
The remote service: contracted connectivity, reception and storage of the authorised subset, rule evaluation, alert delivery, escalation, the history window, user seats and service support. The exact detail is not yet decided and will be published with the approved offer; the device is not included in the subscription.

**What happens if I cancel the subscription?**
You stop renewing and the remote service stops on the effective date after the applicable period. Local monitoring, local alerts and local device buffering keep working, and you can export your data before the remote service stops retaining it.

**Can I install it myself?**
Only if the questionnaire deterministically confirms a documented simple configuration. In every other case professional installation or a specialist assessment is offered.

**What happens if I sell the boat?**
There is a documented transfer: the current owner starts the release, the new owner completes the binding and the previous access is revoked. The subscription position, warranty and history are explained in the terms published before sale.
