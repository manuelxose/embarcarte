# MAREA PROTECT — Solution copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA PROTECT: onboard battery and bilge monitoring with conditional alerts

## 2. Meta description
A monitoring solution for an unattended vessel: MAREA WATCH Core plus the MAREA SENSE Essentials kit (power and bilge), with installation, connectivity and a monitoring subscription. Remote alerts are conditional: they depend on power, network coverage and provider delivery. Not a certified distress service.

## 3. Hero headline
Know what is happening on board when you are not there, without promising what the network cannot deliver.

## 4. Hero subheading
MAREA PROTECT brings together a MAREA WATCH Core monitoring unit, the MAREA SENSE Essentials measurement kit (power and bilge) and the installation, connectivity and commissioning work needed to get the information ashore. Every value is shown with its source, time and state, and the difference between "accepted by the provider", "delivered" and "confirmed by a person" stays visible at all times.

## 5. Main customer promise
The promise is concrete: while the boat is unattended you can see from ashore the conditions this solution declares it watches — position and geofence, battery/DC state with shore-power context, water in the bilge, temperature context and connection status — with their source, age and validity state. When a condition crosses the threshold you set, an alert is created and its journey is recorded: observed, evaluated, created, queued, accepted by the provider, delivered when confirmed, and acknowledged by a person. The promise does not include always receiving the alert, receiving it instantly, or anyone having seen it: only a person can confirm that. It does not include rescue, theft prevention, damage prevention or any remote control of the vessel.

## 6. Ideal vessel and customer
The solution is intended for the owner of a recreational vessel of roughly eight to fifteen metres who leaves it unattended for days or weeks in a marina, on a mooring or in a yard, especially in Galicia and northern Portugal. It also suits the owner preparing the boat for winter lay-up, the owner returning from a refit that exposes battery or bilge risk, and the second-hand buyer who inherits an old alarm or tracker. It is not for anyone seeking a certified distress or security service, insurance-mandated monitoring, a theft-recovery guarantee, remote control of the vessel or a multi-vessel fleet platform — that is a different product.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **You know what is watched and what is not.** The monitored inputs are published before purchase and confirmed at installation; anything outside that list is never presented as watched.
- **Every value carries its time and state.** An old reading is shown as the last known value with its age, never as if it were current.
- **Gaps are visible.** If the boat stops reporting, the application shows since when, what was missed and what was recovered when connectivity returned.
- **An evidence trail for every alert.** It distinguishes whether the provider accepted the alert, whether delivery has been confirmed and whether a person has explicitly acknowledged it.
- **You decide who sees the vessel.** The owner invites people by role and can revoke access at any time; position is not public by default.
- **The boat keeps working without a subscription.** Local measurement, local alarms and local buffering do not depend on payment; without a subscription the remote service stops, not the local function.

## 10. Product breakdown
**MAREA WATCH Core.** The monitoring unit with declared essential inputs: position with fix quality and age, battery/DC state with shore-power context, bilge water where a compatible input exists, temperature context and connection status. It includes device identity, vessel registration, ownership proof, rules and geofence, history, permissions, privacy, audit and the device lifecycle (replacement, revocation, transfer and end of life). The device is bought; the subscription pays for the remote service.

**MAREA SENSE Essentials.** A kit, not a new capability: it is composed exactly of two modules. MAREA SENSE Power measures DC voltage and current so the customer can understand battery state; MAREA SENSE Bilge reports water presence in a bilge. Each module is calibrated at installation and presents its reading with units, source, time, validity state and calibration state. A SENSE module has no connectivity of its own and does not send remote alerts: that requires WATCH, connectivity and an active subscription.

**The monitoring subscription.** It pays for receiving and storing the authorised subset, service-side rule evaluation, alert delivery through external providers, escalation, the included history window and included users, plus service support. Its exact contents — data allowance, device and user counts, history window and included channels — will be published with the approved offer and are not presented here as final.

**The installation.** It covers mounting and fused power for the WATCH device, electrical installation of the power and bilge modules, sealing and cable routing, calibration, commissioning and one complete alert test. It is quoted separately.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is published per input, per sensor model where applicable, per firmware version, per unit and per data direction. There is no universal compatibility: no sensor or device is treated as compatible because of its brand, category or connector.

- **Monitored inputs:** only those in the published documentation for the purchased configuration, with their intended range and calibration requirement where applicable.
- **SENSE modules:** only the modules and versions confirmed by the compatibility matrix; a SENSE module provides no connectivity or remote delivery.
- **MAREA HUB:** a distinct local gateway product. WATCH does not include it, does not require it and does not publish its data ashore without a specific, consented service.
- **No control path:** no command or steering path from WATCH to the vessel exists or is planned.

Before any planned purchase, the vessel and coverage assessment determines which configuration is valid and what remains out of scope. The connection between SENSE sensors and WATCH is an intended path pending validation: no compatibility row is stated as supported today.

## 13. Customer outcomes
- **A week away with the boat in its berth.** The owner installs the device in a documented configuration, registers the vessel and defines two rules: battery below a threshold for a few minutes, and water in the bilge. During the week the application shows the last known value of each input with its time. One day data transfer stops because of an outage at the berth; the application shows the time of last contact and, when connectivity returns, the duration of the gap and the values recorded in the meantime.
- **An alert that is not confused with a confirmation.** Battery voltage drops during the night and an alert is created. The provider accepts it; the application shows "accepted", not "delivered". When the provider's delivery confirmation arrives it becomes "delivered". In the morning the owner acknowledges it in the application, and that act is recorded with their name and time. If they had not acknowledged it, the alert would have escalated to the next person in the defined order.
- **Sharing with the people who need it.** The owner invites their family with a read-only role and gives the trusted technician temporary access to review the battery history. At the end of the season they revoke the technician's access and check the access record to confirm the vessel is no longer visible to them.
- **Before selling the boat.** The owner starts the transfer, the buyer completes the binding and the previous access is revoked. The subscription position, the warranty and what happens to the history are explained in the terms that will be published before any sale.

## 14. Installation
Installation is intended as professional work with a prior assessment: mounting and protected power for the WATCH device, electrical installation of the power and bilge modules, sealing, cable routing and connection practice, calibration and verification of each module, device registration, ownership proof, account binding, definition of inputs and thresholds, creation of the first rule and geofence, and one end-to-end test alert. Handover includes training on what is watched, what is not, what happens if power or connectivity is lost and how to revoke access.

Professional installation is always offered and is the expected route for the electrical and bilge work. Self-install is permitted only when a questionnaire deterministically confirms a documented simple configuration; no validated rule exists today, so the default outcome is a specialist assessment or an installation proposal. No installation duration, price or technician certification is announced: none exists.

- **The subscription pays for the remote service:** contracted connectivity, reception and storage of the authorised subset, service-side rule evaluation, alert delivery through external providers, escalation, the history window, included users and service support.

- **If you cancel:** you stop renewing, the effective date is stated, what stops and what continues is explained, and export is offered before the remote service stops retaining your data.
- **If you delete the account:** it is a separate action from cancelling the subscription, requires verification and offers export beforehand.

## 16. Data and connectivity conditions
Remote monitoring requires a mobile data service contracted separately from the subscription. There is no telecom agreement, no coverage guarantee and no unlimited or worldwide coverage promise; the data allowance, exhaustion behaviour, roaming territories and cost are not yet decided.

Only the intended data classes leave the vessel: position and fix quality, configured input values and states, device health, alert lifecycle data, and account and access data. No audio, video or browsing data is sent, and no commercial profile is built from the vessel's movements. A stale value is shown as the last known value with its age and is never presented as current. A connectivity gap is shown as a gap; it is not filled by interpolation. Position is not public by default, no public links exist in the defined product, and the owner can revoke access at any time.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**What exactly does MAREA PROTECT monitor?**
The monitored inputs listed in the published documentation for your configuration: in the essential configuration, position, battery/DC state with shore-power context, bilge water, temperature context and connection status. Nothing outside that list is presented as watched.

**Does it guarantee that I will receive the alert?**
No. The alert depends on device power, network coverage and delivery by the notification provider. The application distinguishes "accepted by the provider", "delivered" and "confirmed by a person", and never claims more than it can prove.

**What happens if the marina network fails?**
The device stores what it can locally with its own clock and replays it when connectivity returns. The application shows last contact, the gap duration and the values recorded in the meantime, marked "recorded while offline".

**What happens if the power fails?**
The device reports power loss where it can. If the final design includes backup energy, its duration will be published only after measurement; no duration is promised today. The absence of a report is not treated as normal.

**Does it work anywhere in the world?**
No. There is no unlimited or worldwide coverage promise. Coverage and terms depend on the contracted connectivity service, which does not exist yet; roaming will be explained before travel.

**Does it prevent theft or sinking?**
No. MAREA PROTECT reports conditions within its compatible inputs; it does not prevent, deter or recover anything. It is not a security system and not a certified distress service.

**Can it steer the boat?**
No. There is no remote rudder, heading, engine or throttle command from MAREA PROTECT, and none is planned.

**Who can see my vessel?**
Only the people the owner authorises, by role and with scoped permissions. Position is not public by default, there are no public links in the defined product, and revocation is immediate and recorded.

**What does the subscription include?**
The remote service: contracted connectivity, reception and storage of the authorised subset, rule evaluation, alert delivery, escalation, the history window, included users and support. The exact detail is not yet decided and will be published with the approved offer; the device is not included in the subscription.

**What happens if I cancel the subscription?**
You stop renewing and the remote service stops on the effective date after the applicable period. Local measurement, local alarms and local buffering keep working, and you can export your data before the remote service stops retaining it.

**Can I install it myself?**
Only if a questionnaire deterministically confirms a documented simple configuration. In every other case professional installation or a specialist assessment is offered.

**What happens if I sell the boat?**
There is a documented transfer: the current owner starts the release, the new owner completes the binding and the previous access is revoked. The subscription position, warranty and history are explained in the terms published before any sale.

**Does it include MAREA HUB?**
No. MAREA HUB is a distinct optional product, useful as a local combined dashboard, but it is not required for remote monitoring and is not included.
