# MAREA COMPLETE — Bundle page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA COMPLETE: navigation, data, display, monitoring and supervised steering for your vessel

## 2. Meta description
MAREA COMPLETE defines a coordinated bundle of navigation, onboard data, a helm display, shore monitoring, measurement and supervised steering, always subject to a compatibility survey and a professional per-vessel quotation. It is an internal definition: not an offer and not a product for sale.

## 3. Hero headline
Integrate the vessel as one system, with every part accepted on its own evidence.

## 4. Hero subheading
MAREA COMPLETE brings together MAREA NAV Pro, MAREA HUB Pro, MAREA DISPLAY 7, MAREA WATCH Core, four MAREA SENSE modules and MAREA PILOT Coastal — the last always through a vessel-specific quotation. MAREA AIS and MAREA WEATHER are optional and never added automatically.

## 5. Main customer promise
Coordinate navigation, supervised steering, the boat's data, a fixed helm display, shore monitoring and measured systems in one project, with a detailed quotation and acceptance per subsystem. The promise is a defined, documented bundle: not a universal package, not a fixed price and not a claim that everything is verified.

## 6. Ideal vessel and customer
The ideal customer owns a mid-to-large sailing yacht or motor cruiser that is being refitted as a complete system, wants navigation with route context, a fixed helm display, monitoring when the boat is left alone and a clear reading of the measured systems, and accepts a survey, a quotation and a commissioning programme.

The same definition fits a refit yard, boat manager or small professional operator integrating a vessel for a customer. It does not fit a buyer seeking a fixed price without a survey, autonomous steering or remote helm control.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **Navigation and route context in one application**, with charts obtained under their own licence and source and timestamp visible.
- **An onboard data gateway with more integration capacity and installer tooling**, built for boats with mixed-generation equipment and not presented as a "more powerful" device.
- **A fixed helm display** for navigation, instruments and approved status without depending on a portable device.
- **Shore monitoring** with position, geofence, bilge, DC state and temperature, always showing the real state of each alert.
- **Four measurement modules** with calibration and reading state visible; an uncalibrated, stale or out-of-range value is not presented as a reliable measurement.
- **Assisted, supervised steering**, with a physical emergency stop, independent motor-power isolation and manual takeover as design boundaries.
- **Acceptance per subsystem:** navigation presentation, device integration, sensor accuracy, remote monitoring and steering safety each keep their own criteria and gates; none can be skipped.

## 8. What the bundle includes
MAREA COMPLETE is defined with these fixed elements:

- **MAREA NAV Pro** for navigation and route context, as a read-only input to the helm and never as authority over the actuator.
- **MAREA HUB Pro** as the vessel data gateway, with the integration capacity and installer tooling a boat with several coordinated systems requires.
- **MAREA DISPLAY 7** as the fixed helm display.
- **MAREA WATCH Core** with its monitoring subscription, as the shore monitoring kit.
- **MAREA SENSE Power, MAREA SENSE Bilge, MAREA SENSE Tanks and MAREA SENSE Environment** for DC state, bilge, tanks and compartment environment.
- **MAREA PILOT Coastal** as the assisted, supervised steering system, configured and quoted per vessel after a documented survey.

The quoted scope must also necessarily include the full compatibility survey, vessel-specific components, the steering safety assessment, installation labour, commissioning, customer training and a support and maintenance proposal.

MAREA AIS Receiver and MAREA WEATHER are optional: they are not part of the minimum content, are never added automatically and are not counted in a quotation unless the customer requests them. No element is included merely because it appears in this description.

## 9. What it excludes
Excluded from this bundle, permanently or by definition:

- A fixed price for the complete bundle or a fixed-price installation offer.
- Remote, internet or cloud steering; autonomous or unsupervised steering; and any collision-avoidance function.
- A certified, distress, SOLAS or GMDSS service, and any device presented as a certified safety instrument or certified alarm.
- Unlimited features, unlimited data or unlimited future upgrades: MAREA COMPLETE is a defined configuration, not a right to everything that may exist later.
- A single acceptance that replaces or averages the subsystem gates.
- NMEA 2000 certification or operational NMEA 2000 compatibility.
- ECDIS, ECDIS-class or ECDIS-equivalent navigation.
- Nautical charts, data plans, connectivity and structural work not itemised in the quotation.
- MAREA PILOT installation outside the surveyed and quoted scope.

## 11. Optional additions
Two additions are possible and neither is part of the minimum content:

- **MAREA AIS Receiver**, receive-only, for traffic awareness when installed and compatible.
- **MAREA WEATHER**, a forecast service with licence and coverage limited by provider.

Further separate objects, with their own conditions, include additional MAREA SENSE modules, a second control-head station, longer history retention, connectivity plans and a maintenance plan. Choosing an addition improves no compatibility state, no safety state and no price state.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is assessed **per device, per firmware version and per data direction**, and only what has a recorded test is declared compatible. Today no compatibility relationship for this bundle is proven:

- HUB data publication to MAREA NAV and MAREA DISPLAY is planned, not tested.
- MAREA NAV navigation presentation on MAREA DISPLAY is planned, not tested; no interface contract is implemented.
- Read-only MAREA PILOT status in MAREA DISPLAY is unknown: no interface is defined.
- MAREA SENSE measurement to the HUB is planned, not tested.
- MAREA SENSE measurement to MAREA WATCH is planned, not tested; the monitoring service does not exist.
- AIS reception to the HUB is planned, not tested.
- Signal K is not presented as a blanket compatibility guarantee, and NMEA 2000 is not implemented.

The full compatibility survey and questionnaire are the only way to know what a specific boat really supports.

## 13. Customer outcomes
The intended outcome is a vessel where navigation, instrument data, the helm display, shore monitoring, measured systems and supervised steering are specified and commissioned together, with each subsystem accepted on its own evidence and every limit documented.

It is a coordinated, supervised, readable outcome: not autonomy, not a guarantee of alert delivery, not a certified instrument and not a statement of suitability for any particular passage. No subsystem is accepted today and no outcome is verified on a real vessel.

## 14. Installation
Installation of MAREA COMPLETE is **professional and mandatory**; no self-install path exists or is planned. It covers, at minimum: the full compatibility survey, the vessel and steering survey, a documented proposed configuration, mechanical and electrical work, the steering drive and rudder feedback, cabling and power protection, independent motor-power isolation, the physical emergency-stop assembly, display mounting, HUB configuration, sensor mounting and calibration, WATCH mounting and commissioning, per-subsystem commissioning, supervised sea trials where steering is involved, customer training and a documented handover.

No duration, lead time, hour count or installation price is published or promised: none is measured. There is no nationwide installer network and no authorised-partner register.

## 15. Subscription details
MAREA COMPLETE includes two subscriptions:

- **MAREA NAV Pro**, on an annual term, giving access to the application and its edition functions.
- **MAREA WATCH Core**, with a monitoring subscription required for remote functions; its content, connectivity limits, device count and user count are still open, and the connectivity or SIM cost is not included.

MAREA HUB Pro, MAREA DISPLAY 7, the MAREA SENSE modules and MAREA PILOT Coastal carry no recurring fee. MAREA WEATHER, nautical charts and connectivity upgrades are contracted separately, and the same entitlement is never billed twice. If a subscription expires or a payment fails, local steering, valid measurement, local alarms and the safety fallback must keep working.

## 16. Data and connectivity conditions
- **Nautical charts** are acquired under their own licence and are not included.
- **Shore monitoring** depends on power, network coverage and delivery by the notification provider; immediate receipt is not guaranteed and it is not a certified distress service.
- **No monitoring event is presented as delivered to a person** merely because a provider accepted it.
- **Local functions** of steering, measurement and alarm do not depend on the internet, and no cloud-to-actuator control path exists.
- A **stale or missing data point** is shown as stale or missing; a disconnected vessel is never shown as online.
- A **weather forecast** is not a measurement and cannot be used as a steering input.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**Can I buy MAREA COMPLETE today?**
No. It is an internal bundle definition: there is no approved price, no product for sale and no purchase path.

**How much does the complete bundle cost?**
There is no price for the complete bundle. The only intended commercial document is a detailed per-vessel quotation issued after the compatibility survey, naming every inclusion and exclusion.

**What does "coordinated" mean?**
That navigation, onboard data, display, monitoring, measurement and steering are specified and commissioned in one project, and that each subsystem is accepted on its own evidence. It does not mean everything is verified, and it does not create a single acceptance.

**Does it include MAREA AIS and MAREA WEATHER?**
No. They are optional additions, never automatic, with their own conditions and limits.

**Can I install the system myself?**
No. This bundle requires a professional survey, installation, commissioning, supervised sea trials where steering is involved, and training.

**Will the boat steer itself, or can I steer it from my phone?**
No. Steering is assisted and supervised: it requires continuous watchkeeping and the ability to take manual control at all times. It does not provide autonomous steering or unattended remote control, and no cloud-to-actuator control path exists.

**Will it work with my equipment?**
That depends on the compatibility survey, which is done per device, firmware version and data direction. Today no compatibility relationship for this bundle is proven.

**Are the sensors accurate?**
Module accuracy is not measured and environmental protection is not verified. A reading is only valid within each module's verified range and calibration; an uncalibrated, stale or out-of-range value is not presented as a reliable measurement.

**What happens if my MAREA WATCH or MAREA NAV subscription expires?**
Local steering, valid measurement, local alarms and the safety fallback must keep working. Subscription-dependent functions end, and the customer keeps export of their own records.

**Is there any certification?**
There is no certification, CE marking, type approval or sea-trial verification for this bundle.
