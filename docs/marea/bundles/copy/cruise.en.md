# MAREA CRUISE — Bundle page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA CRUISE: navigation, onboard data and receive-only AIS at the helm

## 2. Meta description
MAREA CRUISE defines a MAREA NAV Essential, MAREA HUB Standard, MAREA AIS Receiver and MAREA DISPLAY 7 configuration for coastal cruising, with licensed navigation information and received AIS targets. Product definition only, not for sale; compatibility not yet verified.

## 3. Hero headline
Navigation, onboard data and received traffic, together at the helm.

## 4. Hero subheading
MAREA CRUISE is an internal bundle definition built around four separate products: MAREA NAV Essential for navigation and basic AIS target display, MAREA HUB Standard for the local vessel data layer, MAREA AIS Receiver for receive-only traffic awareness and MAREA DISPLAY 7 as the fixed helm screen. It is a definition, not a purchasable package, not an installed system and not a price. AIS reception is receive-only, not every vessel transmits AIS, and no collision-avoidance guarantee is offered.

## 5. Main customer promise
A coherent cruising interface instead of a collection of screens: licensed navigation information on a fixed helm display and on your own device, the vessel's compatible data in one local layer, and received AIS targets presented as traffic information with their source and age. You keep your electronics suite, gain a consistent helm view, and know exactly which data are licensed, which are connected and which are missing. The promise is deliberately bounded: no AIS transmission, no collision-avoidance guarantee, no unrestricted chart rights and no steering authority.

## 6. Ideal vessel/customer
The ideal customer is the owner of a recreational sailing or motor vessel of roughly 8–18 m who cruises the coast, keeps or wants a fixed helm station, and wants navigation, onboard data and traffic awareness together without building a bespoke electronics suite. It also suits the owner adding a fixed helm screen who does not want a closed chartplotter ecosystem and values an explicit licence and provenance story. It is not for anyone seeking ECDIS, certified bridge equipment, AIS transmission, collision avoidance, guaranteed detection of every vessel, remote control or a single fixed all-in installed price.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **One coherent helm experience.** Navigation presentation, onboard data and received traffic are intended to appear in a consistent interface instead of separate apps and screens.
- **Receive-only traffic awareness.** A dedicated MAREA AIS Receiver supplies received targets; every traffic surface carries the receive-only notice and shows target age and source.
- **A local vessel data layer.** MAREA HUB Standard publishes the vessel's supported data locally, with a local dashboard and diagnostics that do not depend on the internet.
- **A dedicated helm screen.** MAREA DISPLAY 7 is defined as a fixed marine display with day, sunlight and night modes and its own included base software — not a tablet in a cradle, and not a device that includes charts or NAV.
- **Navigation with licensed charts.** MAREA NAV Essential provides planning, a logbook and chart display within the coverage you have licensed; charts are never included and coverage is never guaranteed.
- **Honest status.** The configuration is an internal definition with unapproved price hypotheses; no availability, certification or compatibility is claimed.

## 8. What the bundle includes
The reference configuration is defined with exactly these four products, each a separate product with its own licence:

- **MAREA NAV Essential** — the planning-and-passage navigation edition: route planning, waypoints, passages, digital logbook, export, licensed basic chart display and, with a compatible receiver, the receive-only AIS target list and detail.
- **MAREA HUB Standard** — the local vessel data gateway: it publishes the vessel's supported data on the documented local interface and can consume receive-only AIS output from a compatible receiver.
- **MAREA AIS Receiver** — receive-only AIS hardware: it hears AIS transmissions and supplies the received targets to a compatible consumer; it never transmits the vessel's position.
- **MAREA DISPLAY 7** — the entry fixed helm display: a presentation surface with its included base software for navigation presentation, instruments, received AIS and licensed layers.

The AIS VHF antenna and any splitter, the mounting, power and cabling items, the licensed chart region, the GNSS source and professional installation are external to the bundle definition and quoted or acquired separately. Every product remains a separate purchase with its own readiness status; this page is an internal draft and not an offer.

## 9. What it excludes
MAREA CRUISE does not include and does not imply:

- AIS transmission, transponder capability, own-position broadcast or DSC.
- A collision-avoidance system or any guarantee of detecting every vessel.
- Unrestricted, global or guaranteed chart coverage.
- AIS-derived data retention, redistribution, resale or fleet sharing.
- MAREA PILOT, MAREA SENSE, MAREA WATCH or MAREA FLEET.
- MAREA DISPLAY Large (conditional, with no established size or price) or any display size beyond the entry edition.
- Installation labour, commissioning, AIS VHF antenna, VHF splitter, mounts, cabling, adapters or power items as free inclusions.
- NMEA 2000 implementation, licence or certification.
- ECDIS, ECDIS-class or official-chart-system status.
- Operation that requires the internet for local vessel data or received traffic functions.
- Any autopilot, actuator, steering or remote-helm control path, and any stock, availability, certification or delivery promise.

- **A wider licensed chart region** beyond the covered licence.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is assessed per device, per firmware or software version, per data path and per direction of data. A protocol name, a connector type, a brand or a sibling product never creates compatibility. The published compatibility matrix is the only surface authorised to state that a configuration is supported, and today no configuration is verified.

- **Display and navigation.** Presentation of MAREA NAV on MAREA DISPLAY is intended but not implemented; it requires a NAV subscription for NAV features, and MAREA DISPLAY never includes MAREA NAV or charts.
- **Display and onboard data.** Presentation of HUB data on the display is conditional on a tested interface and a frozen HUB protocol envelope.
- **Display and AIS.** Presentation of received targets on the display is conditional on a tested receiver and a recorded target-presentation test.
- **Receiver to NAV.** NAV shows receive-only AIS targets in both editions where a compatible receiver supplies them; no tested receiver path exists today.
- **No universal compatibility.** The vessel, antenna arrangement and equipment must pass a documented assessment before any quotation.
- **NMEA 2000 is not implemented, not licensed and not certified.** Signal K is not a blanket guarantee. No component steers, drives or remotely controls the vessel.

## 13. Customer outcomes
The intended result is a coherent integrated cruising interface: a fixed helm display showing navigation presentation, onboard data and received traffic; a navigation application with a licensed chart area and a logbook you can export; and honest indications of target age, data freshness and unavailable states. The customer should be able to say "I can see my navigation, my boat's data and the traffic I receive in one place, and I know what each value depends on". The outcome explicitly excludes AIS transmission, DSC, collision-avoidance guarantees, guaranteed detection, unrestricted chart rights and any steering authority.

## 14. Installation
MAREA CRUISE is a physical, surveyed integration: the AIS VHF antenna and any splitter must be installed and connected, the HUB and receiver mounted and powered, the display mounted with its power and harness, the local interface configured, and the four products commissioned and handed over. The intended route is a vessel assessment, a written proposal itemising included and excluded items, installation, configuration, function and freshness checks, owner handover and a permanent installation record. Self-install is only considered where a validated low-risk configuration is confirmed; no such validated rule exists today. Installation duration and price are quoted per vessel after the assessment; no duration, price, stock or lead time is stated here, and no installed-system price exists.

## 15. Subscription details
MAREA NAV Essential is defined as an annual subscription that provides the edition's application entitlements for the term and renews annually. MAREA NAV Pro is an optional annual upgrade subscription; it is never automatically required. MAREA HUB Standard, MAREA AIS Receiver and MAREA DISPLAY 7 are one-off hardware purchases: the HUB and the display include their base software, and none of them requires a subscription. An optional MAREA WEATHER subscription is a separate licensed service on the same account and is never charged twice for the same entitlement. Amounts, renewal terms, VAT treatment, refunds and cancellation mechanics are not approved and are not shown on this page; any published offer will state them before a purchase could exist. On cancellation, subscription-dependent functions end at the end of the period, while your own routes, waypoints and logbook remain exportable.

## 16. Data/connectivity conditions
The intended design is local-first: HUB local data, display base presentation and received AIS targets do not require internet, and losing the connection to the commercial website must not interrupt local functions. Licensed chart, tide, current and forecast layers are separate entitlements that require their own licence and connectivity; offline use is limited to regions where the licence permits it. Received AIS data are used locally for the vessel's own situational awareness only: they are not retained for redistribution, resold or shared as a fleet service. No mobile coverage, Wi-Fi quality, SIM/data plan or provider availability is included or guaranteed. Every value shows its source and age where the source provides them, and a stale, lost or unavailable value is marked explicitly rather than presented as current.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**Is MAREA CRUISE available to buy?**
No. It is an internal bundle definition. The products are defined but not for sale, no price is approved and no purchase path exists.

**Does AIS transmit my position?**
No. MAREA AIS Receiver is receive-only: it never transmits the vessel's position, is not a transponder and is not a collision-avoidance system. Not every vessel transmits AIS.

**Do I need a professional installer?**
The intended route is a vessel survey and a professional installation, particularly for the AIS antenna chain and the display mounting. Self-install is only considered where a validated low-risk configuration exists; no such rule is validated today.

**Is MAREA NAV Pro included?**
No. The reference configuration uses MAREA NAV Essential, which already includes the receive-only AIS target list/detail and licensed basic chart display. NAV Pro is an optional upgrade for live instruments, multi-device, CPA/TCPA, AIS alerts and offline regions.

**Are the charts included?**
No. Chart data is a licensed entitlement acquired separately, with the coverage and terms the provider allows. Guaranteed or unrestricted coverage is never promised.

**Does the display include navigation?**
No. MAREA DISPLAY 7 is a device with included base software that presents data; it never includes MAREA NAV, charts, MAREA WEATHER, a HUB or an AIS receiver, and NAV features require a NAV subscription.

**Can I choose a larger display or the connected AIS edition?**
No. MAREA DISPLAY Large is not established and is never selectable, and MAREA AIS Connected is conditional and not yet justified; it must not be selected in this configuration.

**Can the bundle steer the boat?**
No. No component of MAREA CRUISE steers, drives or remotely controls the vessel, and no autopilot control path is offered or implied.
