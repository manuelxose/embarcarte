# MAREA SAIL — Bundle page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA SAIL: navigation, onboard data and supervised steering for sailing yachts

## 2. Meta description
MAREA SAIL defines a bundle of navigation, onboard data, wind measurement and supervised steering for a sailing yacht, always subject to a vessel survey and a professional quotation. It is an internal definition: not an offer and not a product for sale.

## 3. Hero headline
One coherent bundle for sailing with continuous supervision and the boat's data in one place.

## 4. Hero subheading
MAREA SAIL brings together MAREA NAV Pro, MAREA HUB Standard, MAREA PILOT Coastal — always through a vessel-specific quotation — and MAREA SENSE Wind as the wind measurement. Every element keeps its own definition state, its limits and its compatibility conditions: nothing is presented as included by default.

## 5. Main customer promise
Prepare the passage, see the boat's data, measure the wind and hold a course with an assisted, supervised steering system that requires a person at the helm and allows manual control at all times. The promise is a documented, honest bundle about what is verified today and what is not: it is not a fixed price, not an immediate installation and not a system that sails itself.

## 6. Ideal vessel and customer
The ideal customer owns a mid-sized sailing yacht with a working mechanical or hydraulic steering system and a manual release, sails by day and in coastal waters, wants to hold a course under supervision, read the boat's data clearly and measure wind without replacing the whole electronic ecosystem.

The same definition fits the installer or refit yard that needs a documented, quotable product to offer its customer. It does not fit a buyer seeking autonomous steering, remote helm control or a fixed price without a survey.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **Navigation and route context in one application**, with charts obtained under their own licence and source and timestamp visible.
- **Instrument and navigation data in a local onboard interface**, with the HUB as the vessel gateway and no internet dependency for local functions.
- **Assisted, supervised steering**, with permanent design boundaries: a physical emergency stop, independent motor-power isolation and manual takeover always available.
- **Local wind measurement** with calibration and reading state visible; an uncalibrated, stale or out-of-range value is not presented as a reliable measurement.
- **One survey and quotation process** that names every inclusion and every exclusion before any commitment.
- **Subscription isolation**: no subscription, failed payment or expiry can disable local steering, valid measurement, local alarms or the safety fallback.

## 8. What the bundle includes
MAREA SAIL is defined with these fixed elements:

- **MAREA NAV Pro** for navigation and route context, as a read-only input to the helm and never as authority over the actuator.
- **MAREA HUB Standard** as the onboard gateway for instrument and navigation data.
- **MAREA PILOT Coastal** as the assisted, supervised steering system: it is configured and quoted per vessel after a documented survey of the boat and its steering system.
- **MAREA SENSE Wind** for wind measurement, conditional as a steering input on a calibrated and verified measurement.

The quoted scope must also necessarily include the documented vessel and steering survey, the steering equipment inventory, drive/actuator eligibility, rudder feedback, cabling, power protection, the physical emergency-stop assembly, mechanical interfaces, installer labour, commissioning, supervised sea trials, owner training, conformity work and the warranty reserve.

No element is included merely because it appears in this description: the actual inclusion is decided in the survey and itemised in the quotation.

## 9. What it excludes
Excluded from this bundle, permanently or by definition:

- A fixed price for the complete bundle or a fixed-price installation offer.
- Remote, internet or cloud steering; autonomous or unsupervised steering; and any collision-avoidance function.
- A certified, distress, SOLAS or GMDSS service, and any device presented as a certified safety instrument.
- A guaranteed wind-steering capability while the wind mode is unverified.
- A MAREA SENSE Sail kit as a mandatory item of the bundle.
- Unverified wind data or a generic weather forecast used as a steering input.
- NMEA 2000 certification or operational NMEA 2000 compatibility.
- ECDIS, ECDIS-class or ECDIS-equivalent navigation.
- Guaranteed suitability for every sailing yacht, every steering system or every passage.
- Nautical charts, data plans, connectivity and structural work not itemised in the quotation.

## 11. Optional additions
No addition is part of the bundle and none is enabled by default. They can be contracted separately, each with its own conditions and limits:

- **MAREA AIS Receiver**, receive-only, for traffic awareness when installed and compatible.
- **MAREA WEATHER**, a forecast service with licence and coverage limited by provider.
- **MAREA DISPLAY 7**, a fixed helm display with mounting and installation quoted separately.
- **Further MAREA SENSE modules**, and a second control-head station where the survey supports it.
- **A maintenance and support plan**, as a separately quoted service.

Choosing an addition improves no compatibility state, no safety state and no price state, and does not turn the bundle into another product.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is assessed **per device, per firmware version and per data direction**, and only what has a recorded test is declared compatible. Today no compatibility relationship for this bundle is proven:

- Route context integration between the helm and MAREA NAV is planned, not tested.
- Data and status integration between MAREA HUB and MAREA PILOT is planned, not tested.
- MAREA SENSE Wind as a steering input is conditional: it requires verified calibration and a recorded wind-mode trial, in that order.
- Signal K is not presented as a blanket compatibility guarantee.
- NMEA 2000 is not implemented, and no certification or operational compatibility is claimed.
- AIS reception is receive-only and is not a collision-avoidance system.

The vessel survey and the compatibility questionnaire are the only way to know what a specific boat really supports. No compatibility statement is made without a recorded test.

## 13. Customer outcomes
The intended outcome is a sailing yacht where the passage is prepared in MAREA NAV, instrument and navigation data appear consistently through MAREA HUB, steering is held under supervision within a surveyed and commissioned scope, and wind measurement is calibrated and understood — all in one quotation that names every limit.

It is a supervised navigation-assistance outcome: not autonomy, not a guarantee of wind-steering performance, and not a statement of suitability for any particular passage. No outcome is verified today on a real vessel.

## 14. Installation
Installation of MAREA SAIL is **professional and mandatory**; no self-install path exists and none is planned for this bundle. It covers, at minimum: the vessel and steering survey, a documented proposed configuration, drive and feedback mounting, cabling and power protection, independent motor-power isolation, the physical emergency-stop assembly, mechanical interfaces, per-subsystem commissioning, supervised sea trials, owner training and a documented handover.

No duration, lead time, hour count or installation price is published or promised: none is measured. There is no nationwide installer network and no authorised-partner register, and the availability of a competent installer can only be confirmed through a written agreement that does not exist today.

## 15. Subscription details
The only subscription of the bundle is MAREA NAV Pro, on an annual term, giving access to the application and to its edition functions. MAREA HUB Standard, MAREA PILOT Coastal and MAREA SENSE Wind carry no recurring fee.

Nautical charts, MAREA WEATHER, connectivity and any data service are contracted separately, and the same entitlement is never billed twice. If a subscription expires or a payment fails, local steering, valid measurement, local alarms and the safety fallback must keep working; the customer's own routes, waypoints and logbook remain exportable.

## 16. Data and connectivity conditions
- **Nautical charts** are acquired under their own licence and are not included; coverage and offline rights depend on the provider and the region.
- **Local functions** of steering, measurement and alarm do not depend on the internet.
- **No cloud-to-actuator control path exists**, and only read-only status could ever leave the vessel.
- A **stale or missing data point** is shown as stale or missing; it is never presented as current.
- A **weather forecast** is not a wind measurement and cannot be used as a steering input.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**Can I buy MAREA SAIL today?**
No. It is an internal bundle definition: there is no approved price, no product for sale and no purchase path.

**How much does the bundle cost?**
There is no price for the complete bundle. The only intended commercial document is a detailed, per-vessel quotation issued after the survey, naming every inclusion and exclusion.

**Can I install the system myself?**
No. This bundle requires a professional survey, installation, commissioning, supervised sea trials and training. No self-install path exists or is planned.

**Will the boat steer itself?**
No. It is an assisted, supervised steering system: it requires continuous watchkeeping and the ability to take manual control at all times. It does not provide autonomous steering or unattended remote control.

**Will it work with my steering system?**
That depends on the survey. The steering type, drive, feedback, emergency stop and motor-power isolation must be documented. If any of it cannot be assessed, the configuration is refused and the reason is recorded.

**Can the wind measurement be used to steer?**
Only if the measurement is calibrated and verified and if the wind mode has been trialled for that vessel. Today module accuracy is not measured and the wind mode is unverified, so no such claim can be made.

**What happens if my MAREA NAV subscription expires?**
Local steering, valid measurement, local alarms and the safety fallback must keep working. Access to subscription-dependent functions ends, and the customer keeps export of routes, waypoints and records.

**Do you add MAREA AIS or MAREA WEATHER to the bundle?**
No: they are separate, optional objects with their own conditions and limits. AIS reception is receive-only and forecasts depend on licensed providers.

**Is there any certification?**
There is no certification, CE marking, type approval or sea-trial verification for this bundle.
