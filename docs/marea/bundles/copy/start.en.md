# MAREA START — Bundle page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA START: bring your existing instruments into modern navigation

## 2. Meta description
MAREA START defines a MAREA HUB Standard plus MAREA NAV Pro configuration that brings existing onboard instrument data into a modern navigation workspace while keeping your instruments. Product definition only, not for sale; compatibility not yet verified.

## 3. Hero headline
Keep your instruments. Bring their data into modern navigation.

## 4. Hero subheading
MAREA START is an internal bundle definition built around MAREA HUB Standard and MAREA NAV Pro: the HUB publishes the vessel's supported data on a local interface and MAREA NAV Pro presents it as live instrument overlays inside a modern navigation workspace, with licensed charts where you hold them and your own records kept exportable. It is a definition, not a purchasable package, not an installed system and not a price: every product keeps its own status, its own licence and its own compatibility conditions.

## 5. Main customer promise
Your existing wind, depth, speed and heading instruments stay where they are, and their data reaches a modern navigation workspace with its source, its timestamp and its stale state visible. You plan the passage, follow it on a licensed chart area, keep a logbook you can export, and see the boat's data where you expect it — without replacing the electronics suite and without delegating any decision to software. The promise stops there on purpose: no steering, no autopilot control, no ECDIS status and no guaranteed chart coverage.

## 6. Ideal vessel/customer
The ideal customer is the owner of a recreational sailing or motor vessel of roughly 8–18 m who already has wind, depth, speed, heading or battery instruments of mixed generations, keeps a phone or tablet at the helm or in the cockpit, and wants the boat's data in one organised navigation workspace without replacing the whole electronics suite. It also suits an owner preparing a refit who wants one local data layer before deciding on a fixed display or a traffic receiver. It is not for anyone seeking ECDIS, certified bridge equipment, remote control, a transponder, guaranteed chart coverage or a fixed installed-system price.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **One local vessel data layer.** Supported instrument data is published by the HUB on the vessel's local interface, with source and freshness visible rather than hidden.
- **A modern navigation workspace.** Planning, licensed charts within your entitlement, waypoints, passages and a digital logbook live in one application.
- **Live instrument overlays with MAREA NAV Pro.** Wind, depth, speed and heading appear where the boat actually is, and a missing or stale value is marked as such — never interpolated.
- **Your instruments are kept.** The bundle is defined around existing equipment, not around replacing it; every source still has to pass the compatibility assessment.
- **Your records stay yours.** Routes, waypoints and logbook entries can be exported, including if you stop renewing.
- **Honest status.** The configuration is an internal definition with unapproved price hypotheses; no availability, certification or compatibility is claimed.

## 8. What the bundle includes
The reference configuration is defined with exactly these two products, each a separate product with its own licence:

- **MAREA HUB Standard** — the local vessel data gateway: it ingests the vessel's supported sources and publishes the documented local vessel interface, with a local dashboard and diagnostics.
- **MAREA NAV Pro** — the connected-navigation application: licensed chart display within your entitlement, route planning, waypoints, passages, logbook, export, and live onboard instrument overlays fed by a compatible data path.

The customer's own phone or tablet is the application platform; a licensed chart entitlement for the area sailed, the cabling and adapters required by the installation, and any professional installation are separate from the bundle definition. Each product remains a separate purchase with its own readiness status; this page is an internal draft and not an offer.

## 9. What it excludes
MAREA START does not include and does not imply:

- AIS receiver hardware, AIS targets or any AIS-derived data right.
- MAREA DISPLAY 7 or MAREA DISPLAY Large.
- MAREA PILOT, MAREA SENSE, MAREA WATCH or MAREA FLEET.
- Installation labour, commissioning, mounts, antennas, VHF splitters, cabling, adapters or power items.
- NMEA 2000 implementation, licence or certification.
- ECDIS, ECDIS-class or official-chart-system status.
- Guaranteed, unlimited or global chart coverage.
- Operation that requires the internet for local vessel data functions.
- Any autopilot, actuator, steering or remote-helm control path.
- Charts, weather, tides or currents as free features, and any stock, availability, certification or delivery promise.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is assessed per device, per firmware or software version, per data path and per direction of data. A protocol name, a connector type, a brand or a sibling product never creates compatibility. The published compatibility matrix is the only surface authorised to state that a configuration is supported, and today no configuration is verified: the relevant paths are intended but untested.

- **No universal compatibility.** The vessel and its equipment must pass a documented assessment before any quotation.
- **Signal K is not a blanket guarantee.** Each source, path and direction must be evidenced separately.
- **NMEA 2000 is not implemented, not licensed and not certified**, and no NMEA 2000 compatibility is offered.
- **Charts depend on a licence.** Coverage, terms and offline use are defined by the data provider and are not guaranteed.
- **No control path.** No component in this bundle steers, drives or remotely controls the vessel.

## 13. Customer outcomes
The intended result is a boat whose existing instruments feed one modern navigation workspace: the passage is prepared in advance, the licensed chart area is followed on the chosen device, the logbook records what happened, and supported wind, depth, speed and heading values appear with source, timestamp and stale state. A missing source or a lapsed licence is shown honestly as unavailable instead of being replaced by an estimate. The outcome deliberately excludes steering, ECDIS status and any guarantee that every instrument will be compatible.

## 14. Installation
MAREA START is a physical integration, not a download: the HUB has to be mounted and powered, the supported sources connected through the documented cabling and adapters, and the configuration commissioned and handed over. The intended route is a vessel assessment, a written proposal naming included and excluded items, installation, configuration, function and freshness checks, owner handover and a permanent installation record. Self-install is only considered where a validated low-risk configuration is confirmed; no such validated rule exists today. Installation duration and price are quoted per vessel after the assessment; no duration, price, stock or lead time is stated here.

## 15. Subscription details
MAREA NAV Pro is defined as an annual subscription that provides the edition's application entitlements for the term and renews annually. MAREA HUB Standard is a hardware purchase with its included base software and does not require a subscription. An optional MAREA WEATHER subscription is a separate licensed service on the same account and is never charged twice for the same entitlement. Amounts, renewal terms, VAT treatment, refunds and cancellation mechanics are not approved and are not shown on this page; any published offer will state them before a purchase could exist. On cancellation, subscription-dependent functions end at the end of the period, while your own routes, waypoints and logbook remain exportable.

## 16. Data/connectivity conditions
The intended design is local-first: the HUB's local data functions, its dashboard and its diagnostics do not require internet, and losing the connection to the commercial website must not interrupt them. Licensed chart, tide, current and forecast data are separate entitlements that require their own licence and connectivity; offline use is limited to regions where the licence permits it. No mobile coverage, Wi-Fi quality, SIM/data plan or provider availability is included or guaranteed. Every value shows its source and age where the source provides them, and a stale, missing or unavailable value is marked explicitly rather than presented as current.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**Is MAREA START available to buy?**
No. It is an internal bundle definition. The products are defined but not for sale, no price is approved and no purchase path exists.

**Do I have to replace my instruments?**
No. The configuration is defined around the instruments you already have, provided the exact device and firmware pass the documented compatibility assessment. Nothing is assumed compatible because of its brand or connector.

**Do I need a MAREA HUB?**
MAREA NAV works standalone with device GNSS and a licensed chart area. The HUB is included in this configuration because the intended outcome depends on the vessel's own data being published locally; it never includes a NAV licence by itself.

**Why MAREA NAV Pro and not Essential?**
Live onboard instrument overlays are a NAV Pro capability. Essential is a documented lower-cost variant, but it does not deliver the overlays that define this bundle.

**Are charts included?**
No. Chart data is a licensed entitlement acquired separately, with the coverage and terms the provider allows. Guaranteed or unlimited coverage is never promised.

**What hardware is not included?**
AIS receiver, display, pilot, sensors, monitoring, fleet services, mounting, antennas, cabling, adapters and installation are all outside this bundle definition.

**Can it steer the boat?**
No. No component of MAREA START steers, drives or remotely controls the vessel, and no autopilot control path is offered or implied.

**What happens if data is missing or stale?**
The interface shows the state and age explicitly, and an unavailable value is never replaced by an estimate. Navigation remains the skipper's responsibility with the charts and equipment carried on board.
