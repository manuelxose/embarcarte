# MAREA FLEET package — Package page copy (EN)

## 1. SEO title
MAREA FLEET package: a professional fleet solution with software, onboarding and optional equipment

## 2. Meta description
MAREA FLEET package combines the MAREA FLEET product family (a per-vessel subscription) with onboarding, vessel registration, training, device enrollment and support, and adds optional equipment and connectivity under separate quotation. Internal definition, not for sale. Telemetry is read-only and never allows steering the vessel.

## 3. Hero headline
A complete fleet solution with every piece written down and nothing silently included.

## 4. Hero subheading
The MAREA FLEET product family provides the per-vessel operations software. The MAREA FLEET package adds the professional work and, only if chosen, onboard equipment and connectivity. Each element has its own line, its own scope and its own state: nothing is presented as included unless it is written.

## 5. Main customer promise
One organisation, all its vessels and an operational record that does not leave when the crew does. The MAREA FLEET package brings together the operations software, onboarding, training, device enrollment and support, and lets an organisation add compatible equipment and connectivity as separate purchases. Any telemetry shown is read-only, with its source and age, and a vessel that stops reporting is never shown as online.

## 6. Ideal vessel/customer
It is defined for professional organisations that operate or manage several vessels and share shore staff: charter companies, sailing schools, marinas, marine maintenance providers, small vessel management firms and professional operators with several vessels. The usual buyer is the owner or director; the daily user is the fleet manager, the technical manager or the base manager. It is not designed for an individual owner with one vessel, for deep-sea shipping, for command-and-control use, or for anyone seeking to steer the vessel remotely.

## 7. Principal benefits
- **One organisation with real roles.** Eight defined roles, per-vessel permissions, invitations that expire and immediate revocation, instead of a shared login.
- **One record per vessel.** Equipment, maintenance, work orders, documents, incidents and events stay attached to the same vessel.
- **Documents and expiry in view.** Every certificate or policy has an issuer, number, validity and version; an expired document is shown as expired.
- **Honest, read-only telemetry.** Where an authorised source exists, data appears with its source, time and age; without data the vessel is shown offline.
- **Per-vessel cost that reconciles.** Each vessel has its own subscription, start date, renewal and cost owner.
- **The data belongs to the organisation and can be exported.** The organisation can review who accessed what and take its information with it.

## 8. What the bundle includes
The MAREA FLEET package includes, as explicit lines and with a separate quotation where applicable:
- The required subscription to the MAREA FLEET product family, per vessel per month, in the `MAREA FLEET Start` or `MAREA FLEET Business` edition; `MAREA FLEET Enterprise` only as a contracted tier.
- Organisation onboarding: verification of the organisation and its first administrator, profile, contacts and operational settings.
- Vessel registration and initial data migration from a spreadsheet or an existing system, within a defined baseline scope.
- Personnel, permission and user setup within the eight-role model.
- Training for administrators and daily users, in Spanish and English.
- Device enrollment for each connected vessel: device identity binding, ownership verification and a telemetry credential scoped to the organisation.
- The professional support entitlement of the purchased edition.

Onboard equipment (MAREA WATCH Core, MAREA HUB Standard and MAREA SENSE modules) and installation are optional and bought separately. Cellular connectivity and data are an external service required for each connected device. Onboarding, registration, training and enrollment are professional services: they are quoted separately and are not included in the per-vessel subscription.

## 9. What it excludes
The MAREA FLEET package does not include:
- Hardware included by default or inside the per-vessel fee. No device is given away with the subscription.
- Connectivity, SIM or data plans inside the subscription, the hardware or the installation.
- The MAREA NAV, MAREA AIS, MAREA DISPLAY, MAREA WEATHER and MAREA PILOT products. They are separate purchases; MAREA PILOT is also permanently excluded because the MAREA FLEET product family never steers the vessel.
- Nautical charts or third-party data subject to a licence.
- A fixed package price, an onboarding price or an already drafted enterprise contract.
- A service level, an availability figure or a security certification.
- Customer references, logos, testimonials or customer production data.
- A distress, rescue, SOLAS or GMDSS service.
- A safety-management system, a classification-society approval or a regulatory certificate.
- Remote control, steering, throttle, motor or any command path to the vessel.
- A public fleet map, a public vessel directory or access between different organisations.

## 10. Product breakdown
The MAREA FLEET product family is the software: a multi-tenant platform for one organisation with its vessels, people, roles, maintenance, documents, incidents, reports, export and audit, plus read-only telemetry when an authorised source exists. It is sold per vessel per month, VAT excluded under the working hypothesis, and has three editions: `MAREA FLEET Start` for small fleets, `MAREA FLEET Business` for mid-sized fleets needing workflows, reporting and integrations, and `MAREA FLEET Enterprise` as a contracted tier with no standard price and no invented service level.

The MAREA FLEET package is the commercial composition: the subscription above plus the required professional services and, only if chosen, compatible equipment, installation and external connectivity. The package is never a second SaaS and never a tenth product family. Buying the subscription does not buy the package; buying the package always contains a subscription identified as a line.

- **Installation and commissioning**, quoted per vessel or per site after an assessment.
- **Maintenance and service setup**, quoted separately.
- **Additional vessels** beyond the published edition allowance.
- **Extended data migration** beyond the baseline scope and **additional training**.
- **Contracted integrations** and, only if measurable and contracted, a service-level agreement.

No addition is presented as included, none has a new commercial name and none has an approved price. Optional hardware availability is not selected and connectivity terms do not yet exist.

## 12. Compatibility conditions
Compatibility is published per source, per device, per firmware version where applicable and per data direction. Today no compatibility row is confirmed as supported: the programme compatibility vocabulary applies, and no brand, connector or protocol name creates compatibility by itself.

- **MAREA WATCH:** may supply read-only telemetry and alerts to the organisation where a MAREA WATCH service exists and the organisation authorises it. Buying the MAREA FLEET subscription does not create a MAREA WATCH service.
- **MAREA HUB:** may supply installation health and read-only telemetry when authorised. The MAREA FLEET product family does not configure or update the HUB and does not write to the vessel network.
- **MAREA AIS:** receive-only, and only within the applicable data-rights position; it implies neither collision avoidance nor a transponder.
- **MAREA NAV and MAREA WEATHER:** not included. The MAREA FLEET product family is not a navigation product and includes no charts or forecasts.
- **MAREA SENSE:** only through a verified MAREA WATCH or MAREA HUB path; a module has no connectivity of its own.
- **MAREA PILOT:** at most a read-only status view after the relevant reviews; no steering command exists or is planned from the MAREA FLEET product family.

Any configuration with a connected vessel remains in a compatibility-evidence-pending state until a recorded test exists for that exact configuration.

## 13. Customer outcomes
The organisation gains one place to see which vessels it has, who may see each one, what maintenance is due, which document expires and what happened on each vessel. People work with real permissions and an activity record; managers see fleet status, open tasks and expiries; the leadership can export the information and review access. If compatible equipment and connectivity are added, the organisation can authorise a read-only telemetry feed with its source and age. No availability, certification or service level is promised, and no promise is made that an alert will arrive immediately.

## 14. Installation
The software requires no onboard installation. The associated professional work is onboarding and data configuration: organisation verification, vessel and equipment inventory, role design, migration, configuration, invitations, integration authorisation, testing and an acceptance record.

Physical installation exists only if optional equipment is bought: MAREA WATCH and MAREA HUB are installed and commissioned on the vessel, and MAREA SENSE modules are mounted and connected. That installation is performed by an installer, is quoted after a vessel or site assessment and states exactly what it includes and excludes: mounting, power, cabling, adapters, labour, commissioning record and training. No duration, price or installer certification scheme is announced, because none exists.

## 15. Subscription details
The MAREA FLEET product family is defined as a per-vessel, per-organisation subscription. The subscription grants a time-limited right to use the service for the contracted vessels and edition; it is not a perpetual licence and it does not transfer ownership of third-party data.

- **Per-vessel billing:** each vessel has its own subscription record with start date, term, renewal, state and cost owner.
- **Included users:** in `MAREA FLEET Start` and `MAREA FLEET Business`, included users are not charged per seat as a working hypothesis; in `MAREA FLEET Enterprise` seats are negotiated.
- **Organisation fee:** the hypothesis is that no separate organisation fee exists in Start and Business; in Enterprise it is negotiated. It is a hypothesis, not an approved term.
- **Onboarding separate:** configuration, migration and training are quoted by scope.
- **Explicit limits:** vessels, users, storage, telemetry history, API calls and integrations will have published limits; no volume without a stated limit is promised.
- **Transparent renewal:** date, term, conditions and amount before charging; no hidden automatic renewal.
- **If it expires or payment fails:** the paid service stops after the documented grace period, and the organisation keeps the ability to export and retrieve its own records.
- **If it is cancelled:** the effective date is stated, what stops and what continues is explained, and export is offered before the service stops retaining the data.
- **Outstanding terms:** minimum term, notice, indexation, volume discounts, support level and territorial tax treatment will be published before any sale.

No amount is published: programme figures are unapproved hypotheses and are not presented as an offer.

## 16. Data/connectivity conditions
Telemetry is read-only and depends on a compatible source, the organisation's authorisation and separately contracted connectivity. Cellular connectivity, the SIM and the data plan are an external service per connected device: their cost, coverage, limits and renewal are not established, and they are never included in the subscription, the hardware or the installation.

Every telemetry value shows its source and age; an old value is presented as last known and never as current. A vessel without data is shown offline or unknown, never online. AIS reception is receive-only and not every vessel transmits AIS. Position data may identify people and will be treated as personal data where it does; the data-processing agreement and privacy assessment are not executed and no deployed service exists.

## 18. Upgrade path
The intended path is: `MAREA FLEET Start` to `MAREA FLEET Business` with an effective date, no data loss and the agreed treatment; adding vessels beyond the edition allowance; adding optional equipment and connectivity to an existing organisation without creating a second organisation; migrating a baseline dataset during onboarding; and transferring a vessel or device between organisations with verifiable consent, revocation of the previous access and an audit record. No upgrade introduces a vessel-control path. No upgrade price, credit, trade-in or migration price exists yet, and `MAREA FLEET Enterprise` is never reached as an automatic upgrade from a standard tier.

## 19. Support and warranty boundaries
Support is a professional relationship within the edition's published boundaries: it covers use of the platform, configuration, role and permission questions, reports and export, and telemetry-source troubleshooting. It does not cover the vessel's electrical installation, third-party hardware, connectivity coverage or the organisation's own procedures.

Support access to the organisation is explicit, time-bound, authorised by the customer and audited, and never makes support an organisation administrator. No availability, guaranteed response time or 24/7 support is announced: no signed commitment and no measurement exist. A service-level agreement can only exist in a measured, signed contract. Hardware and seller warranty terms will be published before any sale and do not exist today.

## 20. Frequently asked questions
**What exactly is the MAREA FLEET package?**
It is a commercial composition: the subscription to the MAREA FLEET product family plus required professional services and, only if chosen, compatible equipment, installation and external connectivity. It is not a new product family and not a second software.

**Does the subscription include the hardware?**
No. Hardware is never included by default or inside the per-vessel fee. MAREA WATCH Core, MAREA HUB Standard and MAREA SENSE modules are bought separately.

**Does it include onboarding and training?**
The package treats them as required services, but they are quoted separately: no onboarding price is established and they are not promised as included in the subscription.

**Is it a control or remote-helm product?**
No. There is no rudder, heading, motor, thruster, firmware or bus command, and no cloud-to-actuator path, and none is planned. Telemetry is read-only and never allows steering the vessel.

**Does it guarantee that an alert arrives instantly?**
No. Alerts depend on power, network coverage and delivery by the notification provider. Provider acceptance is not delivery, and delivery is not human acknowledgement.

**What service level does it guarantee?**
None. No service-level commitment and no published measurement exist. A commitment could only exist in a signed, measured contract.

**Is it certified?**
No. There is no security certification, no conformity certificate and no classification-society approval. The security model is specified and must be tested before any sale.

**Can one organisation see another organisation's data?**
No, by design: separation between organisations is a requirement and must be proven with positive and negative tests before any sale. Installer and support access is limited, time-bound and audited.

**Can it be used without MAREA hardware?**
Yes. The software stands alone; telemetry requires a compatible, authorised source.

**Can it be bought today?**
No. The package is defined and not for sale: no package price is approved, no deployed service exists and no contract can be signed.

The MAREA FLEET package is defined for the daily work of a small or mid-sized fleet: knowing which vessels exist, who may see them, what maintenance is due, which document expires and what happened on each vessel, with real roles, audit and export.
