## 0. How to read this document

Every experiment below is classified **individually**. None is deleted, merged, reworded,
re-scoped or silently turned into a PASS. The registered hypothesis, minimum evidence and decision
threshold are the MC-09 versions, frozen before any data could exist. MC-10 executed none of them
because no outreach authorization was granted.

The forbidden transformation is the one this document exists to prevent:

> "The ten experiments were designed, therefore the hypotheses were tested."

They were **not** tested. A design is not a result; a planned threshold is not an observation; a
missing participant is not a null finding. No experiment may be reported as PASS, FAIL or
INCONCLUSIVE: those verdicts require real data against the frozen threshold. All ten are
`BLOCKED` with `executionStatus = NOT_EXECUTED`.

## 1. EXP-MC10-01 — Real problem and purchase-history validation

- **Registered hypothesis.** At least four of the eight defined buyer segments experience a recurring,
  economically consequential boat-data fragmentation problem that they already spend money or time to
  work around, and can describe a recent real purchase made to address it.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** ≥5 and target 8–10 completed interviews per narrowly defined
  segment (n = 40–80 total), verbatim-coded transcript, documented purchase-history answer naming
  product, year, amount band and currency; two independent coders; saturation recorded per segment.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if a problem theme appears unprompted in ≥60% of coded units in a
  segment AND ≥50% of that segment's participants name a concrete prior spend or workaround cost.
  FAIL if the theme appears in ≤20% and no current spend is evidenced. Between 20% and 60% =
  INCONCLUSIVE requiring a quantitative stage. No threshold may be relaxed after fielding.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Unprompted problems, frequency, incidents, workarounds,
  purchases, spend bands and rejected alternatives: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`, not INCONCLUSIVE and not PASS. No coded unit exists, so no percentage has
  a denominator.
- **What would count.** A real eligible participant, a completed interview record with consent, a
  verbatim problem description offered before concept exposure, a dated purchase record with amount
  band and currency, and a valid coded unit.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W1 with SEG-01/02/03 first.

## 2. EXP-MC10-02 — Installer compatibility and liability

- **Registered hypothesis.** Marine-electronics installers will not fit an unproven multi-brand
  gateway or sensor system without a published compatibility matrix, a documented commissioning
  procedure, a named warranty/liability position and a rework path; their objections, not boat-owner
  enthusiasm, are the binding constraint on refit-led adoption.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Minimum 5 completed interviews with installers having ≥5 years of
  refit experience and ≥2 first-party brands in the last 24 months; each must produce a named
  go/no-go requirement list and a liability/insurance statement in their own words.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥70% of installers independently name compatibility evidence and
  warranty/RMA clarity as mandatory before fitting, and ≥50% say a documented checklist plus named
  liability position would make them willing to run a supervised trial install. FAIL if the majority
  say no documentation would change their position. Documented signed trial intent is a separate
  later test; verbal interest alone is not evidence of a trial.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Compatibility documentation demands, warranty/RMA expectations,
  liability/insurance positions, rework concerns, training needs and trial stance: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. No installer was contacted.
- **What would count.** A real installer profile, a recorded go/no-go list, a stated liability
  position, and a categorical (never verbal-commitment) trial stance.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W1/W3.

## 3. EXP-MC10-03 — Subscription acceptance

- **Registered hypothesis.** Owners and small operators reject an always-on subscription for locally
  available functions but will consider a subscription for genuinely continuous external services
  (forecast data, remote monitoring, connectivity), provided local safety and navigation functions keep
  working without it.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** All interviewed participants complete the subscription-attitude
  block; ≥5 objection codes per segment and at least one unprompted alternative payment model per
  participant; Van Westendorp subscription module completed by every participant (n = 5–10 per segment,
  indicative only).
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥50% of a segment names at least one external-service subscription
  they would consider AND ≥50% state that loss of local functions would be a blocking objection that
  must be answered. FAIL if a majority reject any recurring charge regardless of service scope.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Subscription attitudes, acceptable bands, lockout reactions,
  cancellation/export expectations and entitlement-sharing answers: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. No subscription result exists, therefore no subscription model is endorsed,
  and no local safety-critical function may be disabled on the strength of a result that does not exist.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W2, only after comprehension gating.

## 4. EXP-MC10-04 — Gabor–Granger stated-price sensitivity

- **Registered hypothesis.** Purchase intent for a reference NAV+HUB configuration and for a
  monitoring configuration declines gradually rather than collapsing across the tested price ladder,
  and the 50% intent band differs materially between segments and channels.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Gabor–Granger ladder with ≥5 price points per configuration
  (hypothesis figures only, never shown as orderable); every participant answers all points; n ≥ 8
  per segment per configuration; buy-response scale fixed before fielding; ladders derived from MC-09
  price observations.
- **Registered threshold.** Indicative only. PASS for progression if data are internally consistent
  (monotone decline in ≥4 of 5 ladders, no participant answering in under 60 seconds) and produce a
  testable range for a later quantitative study with n ≥ 100 per segment. FAIL if non-monotone or
  unusable responses in the majority of ladders. No price may be approved from this experiment.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Administered price points: **0**; responses: **0**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. The frozen ladders exist as hypotheses in `pricing/MC10-GABOR-GRANGER.md`
  and `data/pricing-ladders.json`; no ladder was administered.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W2 with the frozen ladder only.

## 5. EXP-MC10-05 — Van Westendorp price perception

- **Registered hypothesis.** Owners and operators hold a coherent acceptable price range for
  navigation/weather software and for monitoring hardware-plus-subscription, and their too-cheap,
  bargain, expensive and too-expensive points cluster by segment rather than randomly.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** All four Van Westendorp questions answered per construct; n ≥ 8
  per segment per construct; consistency check (too cheap < bargain < expensive < too expensive)
  enforced and violations recorded, not silently corrected.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥70% of responses are internally consistent and the sample
  produces a non-degenerate acceptable range bounded by ≥2 responses on each side. FAIL if fewer
  than 50% are consistent, or answers are uninterpretable because participants cannot separate
  hardware, software and installation cost.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Modules administered: **0**; raw price answers: **0**; derived
  ranges: none.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. `pricing/MC10-VAN-WESTENDORP.md` contains the design and no findings.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W2.

## 6. EXP-MC10-06 — Bundle comprehension and exclusions

- **Registered hypothesis.** After neutral concept descriptions, owners and operators can correctly
  restate what each bundle does and, critically, what it excludes (charts, installation, transponder
  transmission, collision avoidance, assured connectivity) without being prompted.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Unaided recall of composition and exclusions for ≥3 bundle
  concepts; two independent coders scoring against a fixed rubric; n ≥ 5 per segment; verbatim
  transcript of the explanation step retained.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥70% of participants correctly restate ≥2 exclusions per concept
  AND ≤20% hold a critical misconception. FAIL if >40% hold a critical misconception on any concept,
  which blocks public copy for that concept. Between = INCONCLUSIVE requiring copy revision and retest
  with a new sample.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Concepts shown: **0**; comprehension scores: **0**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. `MC10-CONCEPT-COMPREHENSION.md` contains the neutral descriptions, rubric
  and empty score table. No public copy is validated.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W1 before any pricing discussion.

## 7. EXP-MC10-07 — B2B fleet willingness to trial

- **Registered hypothesis.** Small fleet managers and charter operators will consider a supervised,
  time-boxed operational trial of monitoring or fleet software and can articulate a concrete success
  criterion, but will not commit to a paying contract before installation, support and data-processing
  terms are defined.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Minimum 5 interviews with operators responsible for ≥5 vessels;
  ≥3 must state a measurable trial success criterion. Recorded trial interest is categorical and
  conditional, never a signed contract.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥60% of operators state a concrete success criterion AND ≥50%
  would accept a supervised trial with written data-processing terms. FAIL if operators decline any
  trial before a purchase-ready product exists. A letter of intent is required before any pilot is
  called agreed.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Fleets, current tools, workflows, data requirements, security
  concerns, procurement paths and trial prerequisites: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. `professionals/MC10-CHANNEL-AND-FLEET-DISCOVERY.md` is a design, not a result.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W3. No trial may be represented as executed
  in MC-10 under any circumstance.

## 8. EXP-MC10-08 — Retail and distribution conditions

- **Registered hypothesis.** Marine retailers and chandlers will stock or display a marine data
  gateway/sensor product only if it is demonstrable, has a clear returns and warranty process, and
  does not require them to provide technical support they cannot staff.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Minimum 5 interviews with owners or buyers authorized to decide
  ranging; each must state margin expectation, returns handling and support boundary in their own words.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥60% name a condition they could meet at launch (demo unit,
  documented returns, staff training) and none identifies an unmet condition MAREA cannot satisfy.
  FAIL if retailers require exclusivity, guaranteed sell-through or funded in-store support the
  programme cannot offer.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Selection criteria, onboarding, margin expectations, returns and
  support boundaries: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. No store was visited and no buyer was contacted.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W3.

## 9. EXP-MC10-09 — Unattended-vessel monitoring needs

- **Registered hypothesis.** Owners of unattended vessels and marina/shipyard operators share the
  operational problem (knowing whether a boat is safe afloat) but value different evidence: owners
  want bilge/power/geofence alerts, operators want work-list and handover information rather than raw
  sensor feeds.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Minimum 5 owner interviews and 3 operator interviews; each
  participant must describe a concrete recent incident or near-miss with an unattended vessel and what
  information they lacked.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if ≥60% of owners name at least one alert they would act on AND ≥2
  operators name a handover or work-list information need. FAIL if monitoring is considered a
  marina-provided service owners will not pay for separately.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Incidents, near-misses, manual checking routines, escalation
  paths, coverage reality and privacy concerns: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. No monitoring price is validated, no marina partnership exists, no alert
  guarantee is implied.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then W1/W3.

## 10. EXP-MC10-10 — Trust, warranty and data-ownership barriers

- **Registered hypothesis.** Trust, warranty and data-ownership concerns (who owns vessel data, what
  happens on subscription lapse, who is liable for an installation fault, whether the brand will exist
  in five years) block purchase decisions more often than feature gaps do.
- **Registered minimum evidence.** Every participant ranked or stated the top-3 barriers; barrier
  codes applied by two coders; at least one verbatim per barrier per segment.
- **Registered threshold.** PASS if data-ownership or warranty/liability concerns appear among the
  top-3 barriers for ≥50% of participants in at least three segments, requiring a documented warranty,
  liability and data-ownership position before any pilot. FAIL if barriers are distributed with no
  dominant theme.
- **What MC-10 collected.** Nothing. Ranked barriers, verbatim wording, written-term demands and
  comparison incumbents: **0 records**.
- **Verdict.** `BLOCKED`. `MC10-BUYER-OBJECTIONS.md` records the prepared taxonomy, not observed
  objections. No requirement listed there has been satisfied by MAREA, and none may be presented as
  satisfied.
- **Permitted next step.** None until authorization; then embedded in every W1–W3 interview.

## 11. Freeze enforcement and amendment rule

The MC-09 registry is the authority. `data/experiments.json` stores, per experiment, the registered
version, the source path and a SHA-256 of the canonical JSON serialization of the MC-09 record. The
validator recomputes that hash on every run:

```
node scripts/check-marea-customer-research.mjs
```

A mismatch fails the gate. This makes silent post-hoc threshold modification detectable even if the
narrative documents were edited consistently. A legitimate change requires:

1. A dated amendment record naming the experiment, the old and new thresholds and the reason.
2. A new frozen version; previously collected data remains evaluated under the version in force when it
   was collected.
3. Independent review before further fielding.

None was performed, because no data was collected and no threshold needed to change.

**Registered planning defects preserved.** Independent of the freeze, MC-10 records five plan
inconsistencies in `data/research-status.json` -> `planningInconsistencies`: the per-segment quotas sum
to 37-57 against a 40-80 total (PI-01), three conflicting segment minimum/target readings (PI-02), the
n >= 8 pricing minimum being unreachable for SEG-04/05/06/08 under the preserved quotas (PI-03), six
instrument amendments pending (PI-04), and three owner-side instrument gaps including the missing
monitoring incident question (PI-05). MC-10 does not repair these values: it records them, requires a
dated amendment before fielding, and forbids a silent quota reduction or threshold relaxation.
