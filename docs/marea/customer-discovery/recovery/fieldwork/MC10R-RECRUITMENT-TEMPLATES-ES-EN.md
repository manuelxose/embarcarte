## 0. Purpose and usage gate

This document holds the prepared Spanish and English drafts for MC-10R Wave 1 recruitment. It is a
review artefact, not a communications tool. No template has been approved, personalised or sent, and no
recipient exists. Reading a template is not sending it; approving it would require a recorded human
approval that does not exist.

A template may be used only if and when **all** of the following exist:

1. `AUTH-MC10-001` is `GRANTED`, dated and scoped, naming the authorizer, participant categories,
   geography, channels, volume cap, spend and incentive position, duration and fieldwork lead.
2. A data controller is designated and a verified privacy contact exists (currently `OPEN`).
3. Qualified legal review of the lawful basis and the recruitment communications is complete.
4. Participant information and privacy notices are approved in the language of use.
5. The zero-incentive policy is approved (even though it proposes no incentive).
6. A real interviewer is accepted, trained and authorized; the interviewer identity is available for
   the name placeholder.
7. The channel is authorized and, where required, institutional permission is recorded.
8. A dated approval record names each approved template version and the approver.

Until then, every template is `NOT_APPROVED — NOT_SENT`.

## 14. Prohibited content checklist

A template may never contain any of the following. Their presence is a defect that blocks approval:

- A price, discount, price range, promotional offer, payment terms, deposit or reservation presented as
  an offer or a benefit.
- A launch date, availability date, pre-order, waiting list, delivery promise, stock or lead-time
  statement.
- A certification, CE/RCD/RED/EMC approval, warranty, compatibility, support or "installer-ready"
  promise, or any implication of one.
- A statement that MAREA is a registered trademark, a cleared brand, an approved brand or an existing
  product line.
- A testimonial, customer reference, logo, quote, review or case study.
- An incentive, payment, prize, voucher, discount, free product or commercial benefit.
- A promise of future commercial access, priority, allocation, pilot participation, partnership,
  dealer terms or any business relationship.
- Sales or marketing framing, urgency, scarcity, "limited places", "exclusive opportunity" or a call to
  action to buy.
- A request for personal data before the information notice, or a request for more personal data than
  the specific contact needs.
- Any real recipient name, address, email address, telephone number, berth number, vessel name or
  registration number, or any contact data of any person.
- Health, financial, biometric or other special-category data requests.
- A language version without a paired parallel version in the other approved language.
- Portuguese text. Portuguese is out of scope: no PT template exists and none may be machine-translated
  or improvised.
- A version without the `NOT_APPROVED — NOT_SENT` status while the gate is closed.

## 15. Approval requirements and versioning

For any template to move from `NOT_APPROVED — NOT_SENT` to approved, all of the following must be
recorded:

1. `AUTH-MC10-001` `GRANTED`, dated and scoped, naming authorizer, participant categories, geography,
   channels, volume cap, spend and incentive position, duration and fieldwork lead.
2. Data controller designated and a verified privacy contact recorded.
3. Qualified legal review of the lawful basis and of the recruitment communications.
4. Participant information and privacy notices approved in the language of use.
5. ES/EN equivalence reviewed and recorded; no PT version exists, so no PT use.
6. Interviewer panel accepted, trained and authorized; the name and role placeholders resolvable.
7. Zero-incentive policy approved; retention and deletion procedure approved; restricted-access policy
   accepted; secure Layer A/B stores provisioned.
8. A dated approval record naming each approved template version and the approver.

Approval is per template and per language. A change to any approved template requires a dated amendment
and a new version; messages already sent remain governed by the version in force when sent. If any
condition lapses, every template reverts to `NOT_APPROVED — NOT_SENT`.

## 16. Portuguese scope exclusion

Portuguese is **out of scope for MC-10R Wave 1** and is not prepared here. `W0-08` is `NOT_PRODUCED`; the

prohibited contact and a compliance breach.
