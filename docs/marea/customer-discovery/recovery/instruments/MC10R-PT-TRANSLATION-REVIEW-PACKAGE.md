## 1. Status, honesty rules and why this package exists

1. **A draft is not a translation.** The Portuguese text below was produced by machine assistance inside
   the recovery work, not by a qualified translator. It exists so that a qualified reviewer has something
   concrete to review and correct; it does not shorten the review requirement.
2. **Nothing is approved.** `MACHINE_ASSISTED_DRAFT_NOT_APPROVED` is the label on every Portuguese block.
   No Portuguese version is in force, no Portuguese instrument is frozen and no Portuguese consent can be
   taken with this text.
3. **No equivalence claim.** Translation equivalence for Portuguese is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. No
   back-translation, equivalence panel, reconciliation or cognitive pretest has been performed. Parallel
   structure in the Spanish and English instruments reduces but does not remove translation risk, and that
   protection does not exist for Portuguese at all because no Portuguese instrument was produced.
4. **No fieldwork consequence.** This package does not extend the authorised geography, does not authorise
   a channel, does not authorise an invitation and does not create a Portuguese participant.
5. **No invented reviewer or legal value.** No translator, legal reviewer, controller, authority or
   contact is invented. Every value required for a real review is listed as `OPEN` or `NOT_OBTAINED`, with
   the role that must supply it.
6. **External authorization remains absent.** `AUTH-MC10-001 = NOT_GRANTED`;
   `OUTREACH_AUTHORIZATION = NOT_GRANTED`; `FIELDWORK_STATUS = BLOCKED`; `TRADEMARK_LEGAL_CLEARANCE =
   NOT_GRANTED`. The prepared Wave 1 geography is Rías Baixas / Galicia, Spain only.

## 4. Qualified-review requirements

No Portuguese material may be used until every requirement below is met by real, named people whose
qualification evidence is recorded. No agent may fill any of these roles, self-certify or infer a
qualification.

| Second translator for back-translation | Qualified translator who has not seen the forward translation, translating the Portuguese text back into Spanish or English | Independent of the forward translator | `NOT_APPOINTED` |
| Bilingual reconciliation reviewer | Compares source, forward translation and back-translation; records every divergence and its resolution | Independent of the forward translator | `NOT_APPROVED` |

Reviewer records must state the reviewer's name or approved identifier, qualification evidence, the exact
document and version reviewed, the date, the findings, the required corrections and a clear decision.
A review that does not identify the document version reviewed is not evidence. An agent statement, a
passing validator, a pull-request approval or a Lead instruction is not a qualified review.

## 6. Northern Portugal fieldwork exclusion

Northern Portugal remains excluded from fieldwork. The exclusion is a binding condition, not a preference.

- **Instrument condition.** Portuguese instruments are `NOT_PRODUCED`; this package contains only a
  machine-assisted draft of participant information and key terms. A consent cannot be taken, a screen
  cannot be administered, a guide cannot be followed, a price module cannot be shown and notes cannot be
  captured under an approved Portuguese version.
- **Recruitment compliance.** The lawful basis for approaching candidates in Portugal, the Portuguese
  national implementation requirements and the channel authorisation have not been analysed for Portugal.
  The controller identity and privacy contact are `OPEN`.
- **Interviewer capability.** No Portuguese-capable interviewer is assigned, trained or approved; no
  Portuguese training material exists.
- **Authorization scope.** `AUTH-MC10-001` is `NOT_GRANTED` and the prepared Wave 1 geography is Rías
  Baixas / Galicia, Spain only. No Portuguese channel is authorised.
- **Lifting condition.** The exclusion may be lifted only by an approved Portuguese instrument version,
  recorded legal and recruitment-compliance review, an approved Portuguese-capable interviewer panel with
  training evidence, and an authorization extension naming the Portuguese geography, channels and expiry.
  None of these exists.
