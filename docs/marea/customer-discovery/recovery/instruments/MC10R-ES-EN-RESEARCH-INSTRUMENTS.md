## 1. IV-01 — CONSENT-01 v1.1.0 candidate: four separate permissions and de-identification

**CANDIDATE v1.1.0 — NOT APPROVED FOR FIELDING.**

### 1.1 What this candidate changes

The frozen `1.0.0` consent script (MC-09 plan §5.2 ES / §6.2 EN) already asked four separate
permissions, but (a) it described the quotation permission as *anónima* / *anonymised*, while the design
permits de-identification at most and never guarantees anonymity; (b) it did not separate the permission
to collect research data from the permission to use wording, and did not name the contexts in which
wording may be used; (c) it contained no statement that removing a name alone does not guarantee
anonymity; (d) it had no route for public or testimonial use, which is separately governed and blocked.
The candidate corrects the wording without bundling or widening any permission and without adding any
capability that does not exist (no controller identity, no privacy contact, no complaint route is
invented here — see §6).

## 2. IV-02 — GUIDE-01 v1.1.0 candidate: frozen brand-disclosure text and timing

### 2.1 What this candidate changes

The frozen `1.0.0` guide forbids naming MAREA in `Q1`–`Q11` and places brand disclosure at the closing
section, but no closing disclosure text exists in the frozen source. This candidate freezes exactly
when and how the study introduces EMBARCARTE, the proposed MAREA brand, the concept status, the
unavailable capabilities and the commercial exclusions, so that the closing step cannot be improvised
and the proposed branding is not introduced early enough to bias unprompted discovery.

### 2.5 Neutral deflection line (both languages) for an early brand question

- ES: «Prefiero no hablar de marcas ni de empresas todavía, para no condicionar sus respuestas. Al final
  de la conversación le explico quién está detrás del estudio y en qué estado está.»
- EN: "I would rather not talk about brands or companies yet, so that it does not condition your
  answers. At the end of the conversation I will explain who is behind the study and what state it is
  in."

The deflection line carries no claim about availability, certification, price or timing, and it does not
name the brand.

### 2.6 Why this ordering matters

Naming a proposed brand before the participant has described their own workflow, purchases and problems
would turn an unprompted problem description into a prompted one and would weaken the registered
`EXP-MC10-01` and `EXP-MC10-06` evidence. The disclosure is therefore placed at the end of the session
(after comprehension scoring and before follow-up permissions) and frozen so that it cannot migrate
earlier. It also makes the non-sale nature explicit at the only moment the brand is introduced.

### 5.3 Coding and inter-coder comparison rules (candidate v1.1.0)

- Coding unit: the shortest span of participant speech expressing one idea relevant to one code; bounded
  by the participant's turn or by a clause boundary; moderator speech is not coded except as a prompt
  flag; a unit may carry two codes only when they describe genuinely different properties; a unit with
  no allowed code is `NO_CODE` and is counted so the unprompted rate is not inflated by silent deletion.
- Unprompted definition: a theme counts as unprompted only when the participant introduced it without the
  moderator naming, suggesting or directly asking about it. Prompted material is counted separately and
  is never added to an unprompted numerator.
- Pre-registered codebook first: the twelve codes above are the only codes available before the first
  four real completed interviews. Emergent codes are permitted only after those four cases have been
  coded, with a written definition, inclusion and exclusion criteria, a motivating unit, the date, the
  proposing coder and the reason the a-priori codebook does not cover the case; the codebook version then
  increments and earlier cases are re-coded and the re-coding reported.
- Independent double coding: at least one in four analysis-eligible cases (rounded up) is coded by two
  independent coders, stratified by segment, with at least one double-coded case per segment that has
  cases. The selection is deterministic and recorded before the second coder sees the material.
- Independence: neither coder sees the other's codes before submitting; the interviewer may not be the
  sole coder of their own session.
- Inter-coder comparison is unit-by-code: observed agreement, expected agreement, the number of units and
  the per-code matrix are reportable only from the double-coded subset. Disagreements are adjudicated by
  discussion and logged, and the double-coded subset is re-reported after adjudication without
  overwriting the first pass.
- Cohen's kappa rule: kappa is computed only from actual, independently coded, double-coded records. It
  is `NOT_COMPUTED` when the double-coded set is empty or when expected agreement cannot be computed; it
  is never displayed as `0.00` and it is never estimated, inferred or illustrated. The registered target
  for theme codes remains `kappa >= 0.60`; a value below the target is reported, not hidden, and never
  improved by dropping difficult codes.
- The current state is `NOT_COMPUTED`: zero coded units, zero double-coded units, zero analysis-eligible
  cases. The candidate codebook removes no registered threshold and changes no experiment text.

> is not for sale and it has no approved price. **What it is.** A business software service in which an
> organisation registers its vessels, its people and its operating rules and runs fleet operations from
> one tenant: overview, authorised position and status, availability, maintenance planning and work
> orders, history, equipment inventory, documents with expiry control, incidents, operational events,
