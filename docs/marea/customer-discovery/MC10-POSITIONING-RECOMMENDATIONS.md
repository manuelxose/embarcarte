## 0. Rule for using this document

MC-10 collected no customer evidence, so it cannot **validate** a positioning. What it can do —
and does here — is state which positioning questions remain open, which existing proposals must not
be treated as confirmed, and what a future evidence-based positioning would have to establish.

Any sentence in this document is a **proposal for internal review**, not a claim about the market and
not public copy. MAREA is a proposed brand with `TRADEMARK_LEGAL_CLEARANCE = NOT_GRANTED` and
`SOURCE_IP_CLEARANCE = NOT_CLEARED`; the legal seller identity is `OPEN`. Nothing may be published
until those are resolved and the claims are separately approved.

## 2. Proposals that must **not** be made now

1. Do not publish any positioning that implies availability, certification, compatibility, warranty,
   support, stock, delivery, alert delivery or a launch date.
2. Do not present MC-09 scores as evidence of demand.
3. Do not use the phrase "customers told us" or any equivalent: no customer was interviewed.
4. Do not claim that the portfolio is "market-validated", "customer-validated", "pre-validated" or
   "demand-tested".
5. Do not convert the frozen price ladders into a "from" price or a range on any page.
6. Do not publish a brand story that depends on a functioning cloud service, because service continuity
   is unestablished and a data controller is not designated.
7. Do not position around safety outcomes (theft prevention, guaranteed alerting) — `instantAlertGuarantee
   = false` and the safety claims are unauthorised.
8. Do not reposition the brand as a software company only, or as a hardware company only, while the
   software-first sequence remains an untested assumption; either claim would overstate what is known.

## 3. Positioning hypotheses to test in the first real fieldwork wave

Each hypothesis below is falsifiable and tied to a registered experiment. None is a conclusion.

| ID | Hypothesis | Tested by | What would falsify it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POS-H1 | Owners describe data fragmentation as a real, recurring problem before any product is mentioned | EXP-MC10-01 | Unprompted problem theme <20% of coded units with no spend |
| POS-H2 | "One place for my boat's data" is understood and valued as a proposition | EXP-MC10-06 | Critical misconception >40% or failure to restate exclusions |
| POS-H3 | Monitoring is bought for peace of mind, not for data | EXP-MC10-09 | Owners name raw feeds as the value; or monitoring is seen as a marina service |
| POS-H4 | A recurring subscription is acceptable only for continuous external services | EXP-MC10-03 | Majority reject any recurring charge regardless of scope |
| POS-H5 | Installers are the binding gatekeeper for refit-led adoption | EXP-MC10-02 | Installers accept the product without documentation conditions |
| POS-H6 | Trust, warranty and data ownership outrank feature gaps | EXP-MC10-10 | Feature gaps dominate the top-3 barrier ranking |
| POS-H7 | Bundle composition is understandable only when exclusions are stated first | EXP-MC10-06 | Participants restate inclusions correctly but consistently misread exclusions |

## 4. Positioning constraints that must hold regardless of evidence

Even a strongly positive fieldwork result cannot weaken these:

- `TRADEMARK_LEGAL_CLEARANCE = NOT_GRANTED` — MAREA cannot be presented as a registered or cleared brand.
- `SOURCE_IP_CLEARANCE = NOT_CLEARED` — open-marine-derived material cannot be commercialised or published.
- `COMMERCIAL_PUBLICATION = BLOCKED`; `PRODUCT_SALES = BLOCKED`; `PRODUCTION_DEPLOYMENT = NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
- `PROFITABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`; `APPROVED_PRICES = NONE`; `ORDERABLE_PRODUCTS = ZERO`.
- `PILOT_SAFETY_GATE = BLOCKED` — no PILOT positioning, safety claim or assisted-control message.
- `COMPATIBILITY_TESTED = NO` — no compatibility positioning.
- WATCH: no guaranteed alert, no theft-prevention or rescue promise.
- FLEET: hardware not included by default; no cross-tenant access; no SLA.
- SAIL and COMPLETE: no fixed turnkey offer or price.

## 5. What a later positioning decision requires

Before any positioning revision is proposed for approval, the programme needs:

1. Real primary evidence per segment reaching the frozen thresholds (or an honest negative).
2. Comprehension evidence for every concept that would be described publicly.
3. A documented warranty, liability and data-ownership position.
4. A cleared brand and a legal seller identity.
5. Approved pricing only after cost and WTP evidence, with a quantitative study.
6. Independent review of any public claim.

Until then, the commercial positioning remains the MC-09 internal hypothesis set. MC-10 changes no
public page, no copy and no claim.
