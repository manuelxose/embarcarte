## 0. What this document is

The MC-10 mandate asked for the **actual** objections buyers and professionals raise. No interview
occurred, so no objection was raised by anyone. This document therefore contains:

1. The preregistered objection taxonomy (prepared in Wave 0, before any contact).
2. The recording rules that would have applied.
3. The observed counts: zero everywhere.
4. The distinction between a hypothetical risk and an observed objection — a distinction the
   programme must never blur.

It does **not** contain buyer quotes. It is not a ranking of real objections. Any ranking is
`NOT_MEASURED`.

## 2. Recording rules (would apply)

1. Record the objection in the participant's own words, with timestamp, experiment and step.
2. Record whether it was **unprompted** or surfaced only through a probe.
3. Record the point in the conversation: before concept, during concept, during comprehension,
   during pricing, closing.
4. Never convert a hypothetical trust requirement into a statement that MAREA satisfies it.
5. Never record a paraphrased objection as a quotation. Quotation use requires separate permission.
6. Never aggregate objections across roles: installers are not proxies for owners.
7. Denominator always accompanies any count ("x of y in this sample"), never a population estimate.
