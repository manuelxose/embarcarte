## Main strategic findings
1. NAV and WEATHER are crowded price-sensitive subscription categories. Differentiate through *verified local/offline integration and unified onboard data*, never generic statements of superior accuracy.
2. HUB is a promising consolidation entry point if its real functionality goes beyond narrower gateways and installation burden is economically justified.
3. AIS and DISPLAY entry prices resemble or undercut established alternatives; manufacturing, quality assurance, CE conformity, warranty and installer economics are essential gates.
4. PILOT is a high-liability, vessel-specific proposition. Sell with supervised installation/service only after genuinely demonstrated physical safety.
5. A FLEET offer from EUR 49/vessel/month should initially serve small marinas/charter/boat management with a sharply bounded feature scope; avoid conflating it with commercial shipping ERP.
6. Never claim an exact obtainable market size or revenue forecast based on price listings. Conduct primary interviews and collect representative procurement quotes.
7. The proposed MAREA name is not cleared. A marine electronics e-commerce business using 'Marea Marine' exists (https://mareamarine.com/collections/electronics). Official EUIPO prior-right checks: https://www.euipo.europa.eu/eu/trade-marks/before-applying/availability; Spanish OEPM search: https://consultas2.oepm.es/LocalizadorWeb/index.jsp.
