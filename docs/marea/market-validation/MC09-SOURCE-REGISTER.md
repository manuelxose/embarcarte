## Notes on evidence quality

1. Manufacturer and retailer pages establish what a vendor advertises and the
   price observed at a point in time. They do not establish real-world
   reliability, independent performance or market prevalence.
2. Public forum and review signals are `PUBLIC_CUSTOMER_SIGNAL`; duplicated or
   syndicated posts are not counted as independent observations, and they do not
   establish prevalence, willingness to pay or market size.
3. Inaccessible pages are `INACCESSIBLE`: no price or statistic was inferred to
   fill the gap. Stale pages are `STALE` and cannot support a `HIGH`
   confidence claim.
4. A fact that does not exist cannot have a source. The MC-10 preparation
   evidence file records `UNKNOWN` and `HYPOTHESIS` claims with no source where
   the honest state is "not measured / not tested"; their `limitations` state
   exactly what is missing.
5. Original currencies and tax treatments are preserved per observation; any
   derived total is a separate `MODEL_DERIVED` record.
