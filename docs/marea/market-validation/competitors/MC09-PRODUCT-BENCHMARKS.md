## How to read this document

Each section lists observed current comparables with the original price, currency and
tax treatment. `OBS-CMP-###` identifies the observation and `PRC-CMP-###` the price
record. Where a product is a weak or partial substitute, that is stated in the section
rather than hidden in a footnote. All MAREA figures are historical hypotheses listed
only for traceability.

## NAV — navigation software

| Vendor / product | Observed price | Currency | Tax treatment | Type | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orca Plus | 49 per year | EUR | not stated | subscription, official | OBS-CMP-001 |
| Orca Smart Navigation | 149 per year | EUR | not stated | subscription, official | OBS-CMP-002 |
| Garmin Navionics app | reported 49.99-149.99 per in-app item | EUR | unknown | reported range, secondary | OBS-CMP-003 |

**What it establishes.** A credible navigation app with charts, routing and weather is
priced at EUR 49-149 per year, and the top tier is bundled for a year with hardware.
MAREA NAV at EUR 99/149 would sit at or above Orca Smart Navigation on subscription
price alone. Differentiation has to come from licensed local/offline integration,
data provenance and onboard data ownership — not from "having charts and weather".

**Confidence and gaps.** Orca prices are official but tax treatment is unstated and
regional variation exists. Navionics could not be verified officially; the reported
range is not a single price and is not treated as one. No evidence of willingness to
pay for a second navigation subscription on the same boat.
