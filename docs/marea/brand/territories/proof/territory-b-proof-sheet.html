6. Misuse examples (do not reproduce)

transform:scaleX(1.55)

stroke only

two inks
✕ Low opacity on paper — falls below contrast and vanishes in sunlight
opacity:.35
✕ Crowded clear space — no breathing room; reads as a UI icon
0 gap
