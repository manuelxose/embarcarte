It does not attest the technician's competence, product conformity, water resistance or seaworthiness, and does not replace any required inspection, test or certification. Complete only what you verified; leave blank what does not apply.

Do not record the customer's conformity on their behalf and do not sign for them. If there is no receipt signature, say so and leave it blank.

7. Safety considerations

  • Isolate power and verify the absence of voltage before working.
  • Follow the manufacturer's documentation for mounting, wiring, fuses and commissioning. Do not modify connectors or wiring beyond those instructions.
  • Assisted steering systems require continuous human supervision and immediate manual authority. No autonomous capability is recorded here.
  • An emergency stop (E-stop) exists only where the product defines one and must not be shared with other functions. Do not install or replace an E-stop without verified instructions.
  • This form does not verify third-party compatibility, marine-environment behaviour, water resistance or regulatory conformity.
  • Record any safety observation, even if it could not be resolved during the visit.

This document is not a certificate

It does not certify the technician's competence, product conformity, water resistance or any regulatory or certification-body approval. Any claim of a "verified installation" requires independent evidence and is not covered by this form. It contains and reproduces no certification, CE, NMEA, IP or equivalent marks, and must not be presented as an official document.
